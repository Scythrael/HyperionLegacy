What it does. Under the tab row of the Crew tab (Admiral, Captain Roster, Refugees) a band reads NOTABLE CREW N / 50 and lists every notable in the fleet, aboard a ship or not: name, rank, division and the ship (or a dashed Unassigned), a filled star on favorites. Favorites come first, then everyone else, each part in the order they graduated (oldest first). The strip drifts slowly like the Complement strip and pauses on hover, focus or touch (a small paused tag shows); with Reduce motion on it never moves. Tap a name for the record card: the star, origin, buffs, the ship, Open Complement, Send back and Retire (the same actions and the same Retire confirm as the Complement strip). All lists everyone, still, grouped by ship, Unassigned last.

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save. You need a few notables (Graduate one on a crew group of a ship's Complement tab), at least one favorited and one sent back (Unassigned). For the empty state, a save with no notables.

Builder notes (notable band, 2026-11-12)

  1. UI only. No engine or save change. The band reads state.notableCrew; the star, Send back and Retire call the same App handlers as the Complement strip.
  2. The order. Favorites first, then the rest; inside each part the graduation order, read from the number in the notable's id (each graduate gets the next number and numbers are never reused), so it never depends on how the list is stored.
  3. One Retire confirm. The Complement strip's Retire confirm moved into its own component (NotableRetireModal) unchanged, so both places ask the very same question. The record card opens it on top; Cancel or Escape returns to the card.
  4. Send back only shows for a notable aboard a ship (an unassigned one has nothing to be sent back from); the card then switches to Unassigned with the Personnel roster note, as the mockup draws.
  5. Crew lock. While the notable's ship is out, Send back and Retire are off and the card says why in words (a phone has no hover text).
  6. No new glyphs. graduate, star, ship, manifest, pause, info, warning and close from icons.ts. No titles or milestones (notables have none).
  7. Help, Complement: one sentence in the Notable Crew paragraph.
