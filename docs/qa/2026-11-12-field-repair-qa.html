What it does. On an expedition with ground operations, once the ship's hull takes damage, a HULL strip appears under the Ship lane's track (Operations, Exploration, the expedition's team timelines). It shows the damage against the hull pool with the automatic repair mark at 25%, an Order field repair button, and before you tap, where the ship would repair and how long: the faster of setting down on the nearest Terrestrial, Ocean, Arid or Frozen world (twice the Shipyard's time, plus 5m each way when that world is another body) and repairing in orbit where it is (three times the Shipyard's time). One tap orders it; it is free and fixes all the damage the ship carries then. Until the repair is done, Cancel stops it with one tap and the ship keeps the share it repaired (proportional to the whole seconds it repaired, rounded down); it still flies back from a world it set down on. The automatic repair at 25% follows the same faster rule; it is the ship's own and has no Cancel.

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save. Launch an expedition and let it take hull damage from a scan event (a debris field, or a lost probe or cloaked vessel fight). A system mixing a repair world with other bodies (for example Barren then Frozen) shows the faster choice best. The Debug tab (a DEV_MODE build) can speed time.

Builder notes (OC-F14, 2026-11-12)

  1. One new stamp, optional. A new expedition on a system that mixes repair worlds with other bodies carries repairRules: 1; only there can the faster choice differ from the old nearest-world rule. A run launched before it keeps the old rule. A canceled order's leg carries canceled: true. No SAVE_VERSION bump.
  2. Cancel is for your order only. The automatic repair at 25% is the ship's own safety so it shows Repairing with its reason and no Cancel (your answer 2026-11-12: "Ordered only").
  3. Canceled while flying out (before the ship set down): it turns back at once and flies back as far as it had flown, repairing nothing. An order still waiting for a pickup (or given this very second) is simply withdrawn (your answer 2026-11-12: "Turn back at once").
  4. The readout during the flight back shows the repair's share already gone (as drawn); the engine settles it when the ship is back, and nothing can strike the ship on that flight.
  5. No new glyphs. The strip uses hullRepair, shipyard, clock, check, info and close from icons.ts.
  6. Help, Scan Events: the HULL DAMAGE AND FIELD REPAIR paragraph now states the faster rule, and a new ORDERING A FIELD REPAIR paragraph covers the strip, the order and the Cancel.
