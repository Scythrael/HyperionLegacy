The numbers. Base sensors: Aurora 35, Orion 25, Eir 15, Destroyer, Battleship and Carrier 10; the General Freighter, Hauler, Runner and Prospector 0. An installed Sensor Suite adds its points to the hull's. Science crew multiply the total by 1 + 8% x ln(1 + crew / 25) (25 crew +5.5%, 100 crew +12.9%, 750 crew +27.5%, never capped). A Science officer multiplies it by 1 + 12% at Ensign, by rank Cadet 10.2%, Ensign 12%, Lieutenant 14.4%, Lieutenant Commander 17.4%, Commander 21%; a Bridge Module lifts that like any officer (Focused Command +30% on explorer and prospector hulls, where Science is a role seat). The museum, Inspiring Leader, an Explorer hull's Bridge Module (+10%) and a captain's Keen Sensors then multiply too. A ship with no sensors stays at 0.

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save. Have at least one explorer, one warship and one freighter or prospector, a Sensor Suite, some Science crew and a Science officer.

Builder notes (CP-14 and its fix round, 2026-11-04)

  1. Runs in flight do not change. The plan said an in-flight survey would read the new sensors from its next cycle; the build follows the stricter rule instead: a survey mission or short-range run dispatched from this build on carries a stamp, and only a stamped run reads the hull's sensors and the Science lift. Anything already flying when the build lands finishes exactly as before; expeditions freeze their sensors at dispatch, as always.
  2. Explorers clear early frontier gates on their own (your call: intended). With their base sensors, an Orion meets Tier III's 20 and an Aurora Tier IV's 30 before any Sensor Suite; the drive class stays the tier pacer. The Debug wiring audit for the frontier sensors gate checks Tier III on an Eir.
  3. Fix round: Tier II's frontier sensor gate is retired (your call: "Retire the dead gates"). It asked for 10, and every explorer (the only hulls that can launch) carries at least 15 of its own, so it could never refuse. Tiers I and II now ask for no sensors and show no sensor line at all; Tiers III to VII (20, 30, 40, 55, 65) are unchanged, and each still stops a bare Eir. The short-range sheets keep their Sensors (Tier I 10, Tier II 20): a freighter or prospector can fly them and has no sensors of its own.
  4. Stat breakdown. Tapping the Sensors value lists Base / hull (the hull's own sensors), Ship systems (the Sensor Suite), Science crew, Science officer, Bridge Module (its lift of the Science officer, plus the Explorer line under Hull bonus), Museum and Captain, and they add up to the value.
  5. Not lifted by the other crew amplifiers (your call: keep them separate). The Science crew lift reads the Science headcount, and the Science officer lift the officer's rank and the bridge module: a quarters module's division lift (the Explorer quarters' Science +8% included), Crew Effectiveness and the museum's crew bonuses scale neither (the museum's sensors bonus still multiplies the whole total). Help, Sensors now says so.
  6. Fix round: Help timing is truthful. An expedition freezes its sensors when it sets out; a survey mission or short-range run keeps reading its ship's sensors as it flies (its crew and gear are locked, but a Science officer's promotion or a museum find counts as it goes). The Hull bonuses topic says the same of hull bonuses. For the patch notes: a run already out when this build lands does not pick the new sensors up at its next cycle.
