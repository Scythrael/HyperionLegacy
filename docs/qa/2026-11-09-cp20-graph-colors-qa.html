What it does. It repaints the Complement tab's division charts only: the cap bar behind each rank and the utilization bar in front. Default is today's look exactly (gray cap, red to yellow to green). Color-blind safe holds each bar at one of three Okabe-Ito colors on the same gray cap, never a blend: orange below a third full, amber from a third up to and including two thirds, bluish green above two thirds. Monochrome has no hue: a hatched gray cap and a fill in gray shades that runs from dim gray (empty) to near white (full). The Color-blind palette toggle (the row above) also switches the graph: while it is on, Default draws as Color-blind safe; Monochrome still overrides it. The bar's height against its cap and the tooltip still give the number, so color is never the only cue.

Where it is stored. On this device only (browser storage, one key per graph), never in your save. Deleting the save keeps it; another device keeps its own choice.

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save and a ship that has some complement crew aboard (a mix of empty, part full and full ranks shows the ramp best).

The three columns. Claude is pre-filled and read-only: a tick means a test proves that rule (the "how" line names the file). 1280 and 375 are yours; your ticks are saved in this browser.

Builder notes (CP-20, 2026-11-09)

  1. Default is unchanged. The Default strings are the pre-CP-20 ones byte for byte, and a test compares them against the old expression at many fill levels.
  2. Colors. Default: cap #4c5666 to #333b48, fill hsl hue 0 to 60 to 120 (80% saturation, 62% lightness at the top). Color-blind safe: fixed steps, #d55e00 (orange) for a fill below 1/3, #e69f00 (amber) from 1/3 up to and including 2/3, #009e73 (bluish green) above 2/3. Exactly a third and exactly two thirds (for example 2 of 6 and 4 of 6) are amber. Monochrome: fill #8c9198 to #f2f4f7, cap hatched #5a5f66 and #24272c.
  3. Contrast. The game's surfaces are all dark, so the check is against the deep background and the panel tint. Every Color-blind safe and Monochrome fill clears 3:1 there (lowest: vermillion 4.8:1 on the panel). Against the cap bar behind it, the emptiest fill sits near 2:1 in all three presets, the same as Default today; the height and the tooltip carry that case.
  4. Live. Changing the select, or the Color-blind palette toggle, repaints a Complement tab already open behind Settings, no reload.
  5. The toggle link. The graph draws Monochrome if you chose it; else Color-blind safe if you chose it or the Color-blind palette toggle is on; else Default. The select keeps showing your own choice, so with the toggle on and Default chosen it still reads "Default" while the bars are color-blind safe (both descriptions say so). With the toggle off, Default is unchanged.
  6. Help. There is no Complement Help topic yet (Task 34 owns it), so the graph key on the Complement tab now ends with "Graph colors: Settings, UI, Accessibility".
  7. Device only. No engine or save change, no SAVE_VERSION bump, no wiring registry row.
