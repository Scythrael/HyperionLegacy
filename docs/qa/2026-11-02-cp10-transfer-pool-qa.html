Builder notes (CP-10, 2026-11-02)

  1. One engine, so the numbers are true. Every count the picker shows (each destination's room, each row's warning, "Transfer N", the summary and the result) comes from running the CP-7 transfer engine as a preview on the live game. It moves through the one cap gate (notables aboard counted), never removes anyone, refuses a ship that is out, and moves only between manual ships and the pool (your OC-7 answer).
  2. Where the mockup and the build differ: the window title reads "Transfer crew from ..." at both widths; the phone's step line ("Transfer 12 crew, 1 of 2" then "To Kestrel Line, 2 of 2") sits under it with Back in the footer. The ALL button also shows on the phone rows (the phone frame drew minus, count, plus only; your pick 3 names ALL). The hull badge is hidden when the ship's name already is its hull. Two groups that read the same (same origin, division, rank and rarity, different buffs) name their buffs under the row so you can tell them apart.
  3. The row warning names the cap that stopped it after the move: the ship's berths, the division, or the rank caps ("no more Medical berths at Lieutenant or above"). The mockup's "nearly full" wording became the exact cap.
  4. Transfer more reopens the picker on what did not move (the leftovers, still where they were); if everything moved, on what is left of the same groups.
  5. The crew pool tab shows the waiting crew, a headcount per division (stat tiles on desktop; on a phone the same numbers are one sideways chip row that also filters), the same Crew Groups table as a ship without Graduate, a Manifest, and "Send N to a ship...".
  6. Lock marks. While a ship is out, Senior Staff, Away Team and Complement each show a lock line at the top, and those three sub-tabs wear a small lock (Loadout keeps its own gear lock words). The Complement line now reads "crew changes are locked ... Favorites and the Manifest still work", and its toggle says "(paused while out)" on an auto-crew ship.
  7. Unassigned (built in CP-6, named by you) now also shows what each member still wears ("Armor, Weapon, Tool installed, 1 implant"), as option A in the mockup draws it.
  8. No save step, no rule change, no golden moved. Transfer is the CP-7 engine (already tested); this slice adds the screens, the App handler and a pool manifest (the manifest reads the pool the same way it reads a ship).
