What it does. Every running job Overtime is offered on now has an Overtime strip under its progress bar: a hull build, a hull repair, a blueprint project, a data study, and every facility upgrade (including the Docks, the docking bays and the Systems Bay). Each step speeds the job by 25% of its normal rate, at most 3 steps (1.75x). Step k costs ceil(10 x the job's whole minutes x its tier x k), fixed for the job whenever you buy it. Only the time left changes; the job's size and Fleet Admiral XP do not.

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save. Start one of each job (a hull build, a research project, a facility upgrade, an Expand Docks, an Add Docking Bay, an Upgrade Bay, a data study, and a lost patrol for a hull repair). The Debug tab (a DEV_MODE build) grants credits.

Builder notes (OT-UI, 2026-11-10)

  1. One new engine field, optional. A job's speed record now sums the ticks each Overtime step saved (speed.ticksSaved), and a completed sped-up job's log entry records its steps and its normal-rate length (overtime). Both are absent on every job nothing sped up, so those log and save byte for byte as before. No SAVE_VERSION bump (it stays 71).
  2. The completion log is now truthful. A sped-up job's start is stamped at its real start (finish minus its real length), so "took" is the true time; the normal-rate length sits beside it.
  3. Bars measure work done on every job row that offers Overtime, the facility list panes and Command Home. At the normal rate it is the same formula as before, bit for bit.
  4. Two new glyphs in icons.ts: hullRepair (a cracked hull plate with a riveted patch across the crack: the damaged banner and Command Home's repair rows) and facilityUpgrade (a block with a rising arrow: Command Home's facility upgrade rows). hullRepair was redrawn after review: the first draft (a hammer) read the same as the Shipwright glyph (refugeeShipwright, the Refugees tab's hammer) at 16 px. Check at 16, 28 and 56 px that hullRepair reads apart from the Shipwright hammer, both wrenches (tool and the Machinist), the Fabricator and the Hull Rivet relic; facilityUpgrade apart from the Warehouse and the plain arrow.
