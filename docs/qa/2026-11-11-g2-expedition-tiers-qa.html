Builder notes (G2-9 close-out fix pass, 2026-11-11)

  1. Held launches keep the list. The Wolf 359 guarantee counted every dispatch, so a launch to a held system (which draws no new list) moved slot 0 off Wolf 359 while the list seed stayed. It now counts list draws: dispatches less held launches (a new optional save key, heldLaunches, written only by a held launch; no SAVE_VERSION bump, and a save without it reads exactly as before). A veteran's Wolf 359 stamp keeps matching.
  2. Returned candidates refresh normally. Your 2026-10-21 answer, "No limit, refresh normally": a system recalled before it arrived is an ordinary candidate again, so it rolls off with the next launch from the list, like the list's own three. The confirm now says so ("until you next launch to a system on the list"). Resumable systems are unchanged.
  3. Recall confirm for a resumed run. Recalled before it arrives, a resumed run still holds the bodies charted on an earlier trip: the confirm says "Nothing new is charted on this trip; the bodies charted before and the objectives' progress stay as they were." and that the system stays open.
  4. Map labels and aim. Your 2026-11-11 answer "Label + point inward": each hatched slice carries its Roman numeral, small, in the danger color with a dark halo, inside its slice (a slice too small or crowded, mostly Tier II beside the homeworld, gets it just outside its edge). Labels never cover a badge, a dot, the two map labels or each other (tested over 200 homeworlds at every level). The Tier VI and VII sectors are aimed at the drawn core: the core sits in the middle half of the slice, still varied per save. Old saves get the same rule on load (the sectors derive from the homeworld seed; nothing stored moved). A run already flying keeps its frozen enemy territory flag.
  5. Roman numerals everywhere. Your 2026-11-11 answer "Everything": one shared formatter (galaxy.ts tierNumeral) for research tier headers, crafted gear iLevel rows, blueprint tier pickers, Fabricator and Research Lab "unlocks Tier N blueprints", hull tooltips, warehouse tiers and their materials note, Overtime pricing, Short-Range, relics and expeditions. Facility levels stay numbers (they are levels, not tiers).
  6. Wiring. Enemy territory has its own row with its own probe (the same candidate inside vs outside its sector rolls one Combat grade higher); the tier row no longer claims it.
