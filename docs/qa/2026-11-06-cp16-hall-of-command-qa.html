Builder notes (CP-16 and its fix round, 2026-11-06)

  1. Counts start now. Missions and notables are counted from this build on, so a captain who flew for months shows only what they did since. Retirements before this build have no Hall entry (nothing recorded them).
  2. The successor starts fresh. The Executive officer who takes the captaincy is a new identity, so their missions and notables start at none; the outgoing captain's record moves into their Hall entry.
  3. Short-range runs count as missions (your call, 2026-11-06: "Count short-range too"). A delivered short-range cycle adds one mission flown, like a mission cycle or a patrol route.
  4. Lost patrol routes count too (your call, 2026-11-06: "Count it too"). A lost route counts when the damaged ship has limped home. A recall adds no extra count.
  5. The final-retirement warning is fuller. It now also says the slot's share of the fleet-wide captain XP bonus stops growing (each retirement adds 5% for every captain; a locked-in slot keeps its 25%, and only your other slots can raise it further).
  6. Promotions and titles: logged for later (your call, 2026-11-06: "Log for later"). Captains carry no rank or earned title in the code today, so nothing is recorded. SUGGESTIONS.md now holds "earned captain titles" (candidates: the chosen specialization, prestige capstones) beside the captain identity entry, to join the same notables list when built.
  7. Where it sits. The Hall of Command is its own panel at the top of the Archive tab (above the item record), shown only once your first captain has retired. Entries list newest first.
