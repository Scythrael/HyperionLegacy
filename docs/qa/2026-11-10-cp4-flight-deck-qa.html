What changed. Deck Handling (the Flight Deck crew buff, and the Belter racial) already rebuilds a carrier's destroyed and disrupted drones faster on a patrol. Now, while the ship's hull is below half, the Flight Deck's own share of that Deck Handling (its crew and the Pilot) is x (1 + the Flight Deck's Integrity facet), like every other Integrity bonus lifts only its own department's bonuses. Deck Handling from a Belter officer in another seat (the XO included), Belter crew in another department, or a Belter captain still counts as before but is not lifted. The facet is 1% per point of the Pilot's Integrity above 10, plus 40% of the XO's. Example: a Pilot and an XO both at Integrity 20 give 14%, so a Flight Deck share of +40% becomes +45.6% below half hull; with a Belter XO adding its own +5%, the total goes from +45% to +50.6%. It only ever helps: no Flight Deck Deck Handling aboard, or a Pilot and XO both at Integrity 10 or below, changes nothing. It is set when each patrol cycle starts, like Deck Handling itself.
