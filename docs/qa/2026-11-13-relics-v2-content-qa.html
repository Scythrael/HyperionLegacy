What it does. Blanks wait in the vault, counted by region tier and rarity on the Unidentified Relics row (Logistics, Materials, at the bottom). The Research Lab's Identification tab (it was Relics) queues them on lanes (Lab I 1, III 2, V 3, VII 4); the next queued blank starts the moment a lane frees, also while away. A reveal shows the type with its tier and culture, or an exceptional relic named on the spot. The new Cultures tab lists every culture met; at 10 study points a culture's name is revealed with its lore line, and every relic of theirs reads with the real name. The Archive's Museum is now one region tier at a time: the soft cap meter, the tier's bonuses, shelves by rarity, culture samples, and the tier's exceptional sets (Tier I keeps its set ladder underneath). Completing a set writes one notable-event ledger entry (no screen yet) and the log line.
