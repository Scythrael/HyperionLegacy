What changed. Recipes now make physical sense: fabrics are woven from the new Biofiber (5 Fibrous Biomass into 1), seals and grenade agents use the new Resin Distillate (3 Volatile Resin into 1), and many ship systems take Material Lines goods. Every unit a mission delivers can arrive one quality higher on a ship whose gear carries Material Quality (a Refinery-Feed, Yield or Survey Rig). A craft's output starts from the average quality of what it used, and Use best materials spends your highest quality stock first. The Refinery groups its recipes into five material lines, each naming the missions its raws come from.

The fuel chain. The Cometary Ice Harvest (Tier I, flyable from a fresh save) brings in Deuterium Ice, with Titanium Ore and, rarely, Iridium Ore. The Refinery presses 10 Deuterium Ice into 1 Deuterium Canister. Canisters go into Reactor Core blueprints (Balanced 1, Efficient 1, High-Output 2) and into FTL Drive blueprints from Class III up (Class III 1, IV 2, V to VII 3; VI and VII also take Antimatter Cells). Class I and II drives take none.

Builder notes (ML close-out, 2026-11-11)

  1. Refinery sources fixed. Each material line's "comes from" missions were a hand-kept list that missed the Cometary Ice Harvest (Structural: it strikes Titanium Ore and Iridium Ore) and Survey Stellar Phenomena (Heavy metals and Organic). The list is now read from every mission's loot table, so a new or changed run can never drift from it. Transport runs (they deliver no loot) and the retired Local Deuterium Skim never show.
  2. Help made truthful. The Deuterium Ice tooltip, the Fuel topic and the Gas Storage Canisters topic now say canisters also go into Class III and higher FTL Drives. The Quartermaster topic now lists everything the Sell tab buys: Deuterium Ice, Salvaged Circuitry, Precursor Circuit and the gas canisters.
  3. No rule or number changed in this pass. The climb curve's [PROVISIONAL] tag is gone (you approved it 2026-10-21).
  4. Still yours to call (OPEN). The comet run's numbers (40-tick legs, 25% Titanium, 1% Iridium, 40 credits), the classed drives' canister amounts and the reactor fuel tiers are provisional; the sensors leg of audit call 14 (Hydrogen and Methane in sensors) waits on your answer.
