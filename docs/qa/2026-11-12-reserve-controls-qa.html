What it does. Facilities, Personnel Rosters, Crew Pool: under the pool's table, the Homeworld reserve has its own section, closed at first. Open, it is the same Crew Groups table as the pool. Ticked rows show Send N to a ship... and Recall N to the pool...; both open the existing transfer picker with the reserve as the source. Send starts on the first manual ship with room; Recall starts on the crew pool and moves only the crew some berth in the fleet can seat, so nothing goes back to the reserve at the next tick. Auto-crew ships are listed but not pickable, with a note that they fill themselves. Before a Send that leaves pool crew without a berth, the picker warns how many move to the reserve at the next tick. Ranks and racials are kept, and nobody is lost.

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save. You need crew in the Homeworld reserve (a full fleet gets them from the transports), at least one manual ship with open berths, one auto-crew ship, and ideally one ship out on a run.

Builder notes (reserve controls, 2026-11-12)

  1. No save change. No SAVE_VERSION bump; no new saved field. Whether the section is open is a per-device display preference (like the other display preferences), not in the save.
  2. One rule for the room. Recall's room per group and the Send warning read the fleet's open berths through the sweep's own reader (homeworldReserve.ts fleetRoomReader), so the picker can never disagree with the next tick. The sweep itself only moved into that shared reader; every golden pin is unchanged.
  3. The warning with several groups. The mockup drew one group. With two or more pool groups affected, the warning says the total and the number of groups in one sentence instead of naming each.
  4. The reserve is never a target. Nothing in the game offers it; the engine refuses a transfer into it ("Crew move to the Homeworld reserve only on their own").
  5. No new glyphs. home, crewPool, transfer, ship, lock, info, warning, chevronUp, chevronDown from icons.ts.
  6. Help, Complement topic, THE CREW POOL paragraph: the reserve's section, Send, Recall's rule and the warning.
