Builder notes (CP-13, 2026-11-03)

  1. Built to SMS-1. Each Bridge Module and Crew Quarters tile gains a line under its variant line, "Hull bonus on Explorer: sensors +10%", "Hull bonus on Tactician: Security and Engineering crew +8%, injured crew recover 15% faster", every number read from the engine at the piece's own rarity, quality and condition. The install comparison shows the candidate's line for this ship. A new Help topic, Hull bonuses, lists all eight lines from the catalog.
  2. Stat breakdown. The Ship Systems source popover gains a "Hull bonus" line where the bonus moves a listed stat (FTL Speed on a General hull, Extraction Yield on a Prospector, and any stat a lifted division moves). Sensors and unload speed are not rows of that readout, so those two show on the tile only.
  3. Runs in flight. A mission, short-range run or patrol carries a new stamp from dispatch and reads the bonus only with it, so anything already flying when the build lands plays exactly as before; expeditions freeze the bonus at dispatch. Once a run ends and the ship is home, its gear repairs on the current rules.
  4. Scale. The hull bonus scales with the module exactly like the module's own effect (rarity, quality, ascension, wear and its Crew Stations or Crew Efficiency lines), the mockup's "the hull bonus scales the same way". The General guest perk stays the flat +10% G1-1 built.
