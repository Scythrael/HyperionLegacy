What it does. Apply on the ship runs the same action as Apply in Facilities, Personnel Rosters: pinned officers take their seats (an officer already in that seat goes back to unassigned; unpinned seats keep their officer), pinned notable crew come aboard in priority order up to the ship's caps, the rest of the berths fill from the crew pool, and a linked Armory loadout goes on first. Picking in the select never acts; only Apply does. Only rosters with Include Senior Staff & Complement turned on are listed (the facility cannot apply the others either).

When it warns. "Saved" means a roster records exactly this ship's officers per seat and its notable crew (the bulk complement is never part of a roster). If no saved roster matches and Apply would change the ship's officers or notable crew, Apply first opens Unsaved Configuration with Cancel and Apply anyway. A ship with no officers and no notable crew, or a roster whose people are already aboard, applies with no warning (nothing would be lost).

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save. You need at least two saved rosters with people, two ships, and a few hired officers and notable crew.

Builder notes (CP-17, 2026-11-06)

  1. Where it sits. One shared bar above the Senior Staff and the Complement panes of the ship page (not the Loadout or Away Team tabs). The select takes the row's free width and the row wraps on a phone.
  2. The warning text. The crew spec fixes the meaning ("WARN that the current configuration is unsaved and will be lost on swap"), not the words, so the modal follows the facility's Unsaved Name pattern: title Unsaved Configuration, then "This ship's current configuration is unsaved: no saved roster matches its officers and notable crew. Applying [roster] will lose it. Apply anyway?", a note that no one is dismissed, and Cancel / Apply anyway.
  3. No warning when nothing would be lost. The plan's condition is "differs from every saved roster". The picker also skips the warning when Apply would not change this ship's officers or notable crew (the roster's people are already aboard), so the words are always true. Say if you want it on every unsaved apply instead.
  4. No saved rosters. A truthful empty line, not a hidden bar (the facility's own empty hints): "No saved rosters yet. Build one in Facilities, Personnel Rosters." or, when none carries people, how to turn people on.
  5. Status line. Under the picker: which roster the ship's people match, or that they are not saved in any roster (nothing for a ship with no people). The picker defaults to the matched roster, else the first one.
  6. Help. Rosters had no Help topic, so CP-17 adds a short Personnel Rosters topic (saved rosters, applying, and the ship picker).
  7. UI only. No engine or save change, no SAVE_VERSION bump, no wiring registry row (a picker carries no stat).
