What it does. Condition runs 0 to 100%. The bands are Pristine (95% and up), Sound (80% to 94%), Worn (60% to 79%) and Battered (below 60%, battle damage only). Ordinary use stops at 60%. Repair is automatic and free: 4% a minute at home, 8% a minute while the hull is in a Shipyard repair bay, 4% a minute on a field repair, 0.2% a minute out on a run. Nothing breaks, nothing is uninstalled, no run is ever blocked.

Quick Service is gone. There is no paid repair, no instant repair and no "repair now" button for gear anywhere (your 2026-11-09 answer). Repair stays free and automatic; the only purchase is the optional Overtime button on a docked ship's gear card (your approved 2026-11-09 Overtime mockup), which only speeds the repair up.

Builder notes (gear wear step 5, 2026-11-09)

  1. Quick Service removed. Nothing in the UI offers a paid or instant gear repair. Its registry row (Quick gear repair) is deleted, and the design doc records the removal. The mockup itself carried no Quick Service control ("No instant-repair purchase").
  2. Two mockup lines superseded by your earlier calls. The mockup said a weapon "opens each fight at no less than 70%"; your W3 answer is true condition, so the card says "Opens each fight at N%" with the real number. The mockup's Repeat freighter line "settles near 90%" is not shown: the line reads "Out on a run, gear recovers slowly underway and fully once home", which is always true.
  3. Where the Fleet care line lives. Command Home has no Shipyard card, so the line is its own quiet Fleet care section under In progress (hidden when nothing is worn and no hull is damaged), with hull repairs shown beside gear wear (W6).
  4. Overtime on gear repair is built in the engine and registry, by the speed seam's rule (+25% of the normal rate a step, three steps at most, step k costs 10 x the job's minutes x the hull tier x k, docked ships only). Its button is on the gear card (your approved 2026-11-09 Overtime mockup). See section 5.
  5. Combat only on patrols (your 2026-11-09 answer). Patrols wear gear only through battle damage, exactly as before step 5. The patrol use wear built earlier in step 5 (a hub inference, not your request) is removed. Gathering runs, expeditions and short-range runs keep their use wear.
  6. No save change. Two optional fields (a ship's Overtime record and its repair job length), no SAVE_VERSION bump.
  7. Review fix round (2026-11-09). C1: gear repair now settles in exact whole numbers (whole-second anchors and an integer bank), so offline and live agree to the byte at any game time. (C2, patrol use wear's carry, is gone with patrol use wear.) Overtime is priced by the repair job's FULL length (fixed whenever it is bought). The Overtime button is built on the gear card (your answers "Per ship", "One tap", "Gear card only"). The care footer now ends "Repair is always free."; out on a run the lines say wear can outpace the slow underway upkeep (on a patrol, battle damage only). No new save field for C1 (the anchor encodes the bank), no SAVE_VERSION bump.
  8. Close-out fix pass (2026-11-11), your four answers. A paused Repeat patrol's gear screens now read it as home and repairing (bar, card, chip, Fleet care), and the bar never jumps when the pause ends. "Allow Overtime in pauses": the gear card's Overtime button works in a pause, priced by the pause's whole length, and the patrol heads out the moment its gear is full. "Keep the bay rate": gear keeps the Shipyard bay rate until full after the hull leaves the bay. "Make it exact": leftover underway seconds at a run's end or a pause's start are credited at the underway rate (they were credited at the home rate, up to about 2%). "Yes, it's combat wear": the Damage Control Suite's wear when it patches a patrol wave stays, and the registry and Help say it counts as combat wear. Two optional fields (a ship's kept bay rate, a pause's Overtime steps), no SAVE_VERSION bump. Section 7 checks them.
