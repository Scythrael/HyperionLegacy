What it does. Blanks used to be one count per blueprint, all the same. Now each blueprint's blanks are split by quality, Q0 to Q5, like your materials. A Fabricator run works out the unit-weighted average quality of everything it used, rounded down (the same rule refined goods already follow), and its blank lands at that quality. Inspecting takes the highest quality blank you hold of that type first; the piece starts at the blank's quality and the usual quality roll can lift it further, never above Q5.

To see a quality above Q0 you need materials above Q0: a Refinery-Feed Rig (or a Yield or Survey Rig) on a mining ship lifts some delivered ore, and that quality carries through refining into the blank. Turn on Use best materials on a Fabricator order to spend your best stock first.

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save. Facilities, Fabricator (the order form), Facilities, Refinery (the Quality carries into crafts panel under the production lines), Logistics, Crafted Blanks, and Help, Material lines and quality.

Builder notes (ML-5, 2026-11-10)

  1. Your save. Every blank you hold loads at Q0, count for count (every blank was Q0 before). No SAVE_VERSION bump (still 71) and no migration step: the load wraps each old count into the Q0 bucket every time, which changes nothing once done, so it cannot clash with another slice's save step.
  2. Crafts already running. A Fabricator job started before this build carries no quality stamp, so its blank lands at Q0, as it would have.
  3. Same rolls. Inspecting reads the same seed and makes the same draws as before, so reloading before an inspect still gives the same piece. A Q2 blank inspects to exactly the piece a Q0 blank would have rolled, two qualities higher (Q5 at most); rarity, item level and lines are unchanged.
  4. Wiring. Two new live rows, each measured: facility:audit:blankCarriedQuality (a real Yield Rig run on Q2 stock lands its blank 2 qualities above the same run on Q0 stock) and facility:audit:inspectCarriedQuality (with a Q2 and a Q0 blank held, the inspect takes the Q2 and the piece lands 2 qualities above the Q0 blank's; Q2 so the Q5 cap can never clip the move).
  5. Not built here. No quality picker on the Crafted Blanks tab (inspect always takes the highest first, the plan's rule), and no quality on ships or field kits (your 2026-10-22 answer "Out for 0.14.0").
