What changed. Neural Backup rolls for its wearer, when the wearer goes down (it used to roll for whoever fell first). A Fall Back rule now shields the member for that round (40% less damage taken), where it used to do nothing. Enemy Casters can leave a member Corroded (30% a landed hit; armor halved for 3 rounds), so the status picker's Corroded entry can now match. A Science member's own fieldwork from gear and traits grows with their rank (+5% a rank above the first). The Med Kit's text no longer promises a revive. A Security member you hire starts with your older account-wide program, if you ever changed it.

Where to test. Staging, devpreview.crystalisoft.com, on the 0.14.0 build, with your own migrated save. Ops, Exploration (the expedition console and the Tactical Protocols editor), Personnel (the recruit board), and the Fabricator.
