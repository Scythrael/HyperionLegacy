Builder notes (EM-8, 2026-11-02)

  1. Numbers are provisional. The 20% chance per body is a first pass (no answer names it); the 9 power draw is the signed-off modules mockup's sample. The tier (Lab tier 2, like the other relic payoffs) and the recipe (the recipe realism audit's row) are proposals.
