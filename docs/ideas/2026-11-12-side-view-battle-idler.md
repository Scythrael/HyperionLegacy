## Structure as stated (for reference, not decisions)

- Idle, side-view, the main pane is a battle playing out. Setting: sci-fi OR fantasy (open).
- Stages: single target per stage, more kills needed each stage; a boss every 10 stages (a small hump); a much harder
  boss every 100 stages (a wall to overcome; must NOT be called a "wall boss" in game).
- Combat stats: attack speed (example start: one blast every 5 s), enemy types with different damage and attack speed.
- Loot: currency and crafting materials from enemies; upgrade gear; every X stages gear tiers up visibly (wooden sword to
  iron blade at sword level 10; leather armor, copper rings and bracers).
- Classes per character: e.g. assassin with two separately upgradable daggers; healer who can melee ("bonk") but plays
  more slowly.
- A group mode: take X characters into a dungeon or raid; the game controls all of them against groups of enemies.
