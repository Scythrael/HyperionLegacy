Homeworld reserve: Send to a ship, Recall to pool

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, for sign-off before the build. Your 2026-11-12 afternoon pick: "Add controls (Recommended)": "Send to a ship" and "Recall to pool" on the reserve so the player can hand-pick again. Mockup first. Also decided that afternoon and drawn as decided: the sweep runs every tick, and manual ships count as berths.

The reserve as built (homeworldReserve.ts): after every tick's fill, each crew group in the pool keeps as many crew as the fleet's open berths could still seat of its division and rank; the rest moves to the reserve. Auto-crew ships fill from the pool first, then the reserve. Nothing moves crew out of the reserve by hand today, and the Crew Pool tab shows only "Homeworld reserve: N".

Sample fleet (the CP-10 mockup's ships): Kestrel Line (Orion, manual, 702 / 750, room for 48), Halden's Promise (Eir, auto-crew, full), Lumen Drift (Aurora, auto-crew, on an expedition, 29 berths open), Vesper Reach (Aurora, manual, out on a patrol), Ironsong (Battleship, manual, full). Pool 46 waiting, reserve 3,412. Amber dashed boxes are questions for you; the drawn option is marked.

0. How each control behaves (the engine as built, nothing new)

ControlWhat happensWhy (engine)
Send to a shipMoves the picked groups from the reserve straight into a manual ship's open berths, cap-aware per group. What has no room stays in the reserve. Nobody is lost.The same move as the pool's "Send N to a ship..." A ship's berths, once filled, are never swept, so these crew stay put.
Auto-crew shipsNot a destination. They already fill from the pool, then the reserve, every tick.crewOverlay.ts resolveCrew: an auto-crew ship that is home and has room for a reserve group takes it on the next tick, so by the time you look, it has no room for anything left in the reserve.
Crew-locked shipsNot a destination while out (mission, patrol, short-range run, or an expedition until claimed).Every crew change is locked while out (crewLock.ts), the same refusal the pool's picker shows.
Recall to poolThe catch. The sweep runs every tick, so a recalled crewmember no berth can seat goes straight back to the reserve on the next tick. Only crew a berth could seat stay in the pool.sweepSurplusToReserve: each pool group keeps min(its count, the fleet's open berths for its division and rank). After the fill, those open berths are on manual ships and on auto-crew ships that are out (they fill when home, from the pool first).
What Recall is good forChoosing which groups an auto-crew ship that is out takes when it comes home: it takes from the pool before the reserve. (For a manual ship, Send to a ship does the same job in one step.)resolveCrew step 2: autoCrewShip(pool) first, then autoCrewShip(reserve) for the berths left.
Side effect of SendFilling a manual ship's berths from the reserve uses room the sweep was holding pool crew for.
