E5: Refugee Posts

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, phase E5, for sign-off before the build. Drawn from the approved design (docs/plans/2026-11-02-refugee-posts-e5-design.md): evacuations bring home one named refugee; each facility has posts that grow with its level; a refugee whose background matches the facility gives a strong buff, anywhere else a small one; moving people is free; nobody is ever lost. Every number is a [PROPOSAL] for the balance pass: skill 20 to 50 from Tier I, 40 to 75 from Tier II; a matched post gives skill / 10 percent, an unmatched one a quarter of that; posts open at Levels 1, 4, 7, 10 and so on; several on one facility stack on the D23 curve. Amber dashed boxes are open calls; the drawn option is marked.

Background to facility mapping [PROPOSAL]: Refiner Refinery (refine speed) · Fuel handler Fuel Depot (fleet reach) · Dispatcher Mission Control (short-range run speed) · Scholar Research Lab (research speed) · Machinist Fabricator (craft speed) · Shipwright Shipyard (build speed) · Salvager Salvage Bay (salvage yield) · Stargazer Subspace Telescope (scan range).

Open calls, in one list

  1. Posts row placement: A strip under each dashboard pane (drawn) or B a chip on the pane with the row on the console's Overview.
  2. Tapping a filled post: A details popover with Move and Back to waiting list (drawn) or B straight to the picker.
  3. Buff readout: A total plus each share (drawn) or B total only.
  4. Mission Control's posts: A on its Operations tab (suggested) or B a new Facilities pane.
  5. Phone post rows: A one line per post (drawn) or B faces only.
  6. Posted people in the picker: A "Posted elsewhere" with Move here (drawn) or B waiting list only.
  7. Gain line: A gain plus new total or their home facility's gain (drawn) or B gain only.
  8. Waiting list home: A a Refugees sub-tab (drawn) or B a section in Personnel Rosters.
  9. Slowing arrivals: A words only (drawn) or B words plus the chance.
  10. Post... on a full facility: A allow a swap or B open posts only.
  11. Arrival moment: A run card with one-tap Post (drawn) or B toast and log only.
  12. A run with no arrival: A a neutral log line (drawn) or B no mention.
  13. Personnel before the first arrival: A no tab (drawn) or B the tab with a calm line.
  14. First-arrival hint: A one-time hint (drawn) or B none.

Portraits are simple SVG silhouettes (four hair shapes, eight color sets), not the officer art; that keeps refugees visibly distinct from Senior Staff. All names are original samples; the build draws them from the seeded crew name generator.
