CP-10: Transfer, Crew Pool, Lock Badges, Unassigned

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, Tasks 25 and 27 UI part 2, for sign-off. Already decided and drawn as decided: transfers need manual crew on both ends, and the picker offers the switch (OC-7); the crew pool lives in Personnel Rosters (OC-10); never evict, an over-cap or full ship simply takes no more (OC-6); a ship that is out locks every crew change (CP-6). Amber dashed boxes are open calls; the drawn option is marked.
