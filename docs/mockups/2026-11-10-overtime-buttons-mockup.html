Overtime buttons on running jobs

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, the OT-UI slice, for sign-off before the build. It follows the approved gear repair strip (2026-11-09) and the built one (EquipmentTooltip.svelte .et-ot, gearWearDisplay.ts gearOvertimeStrip): one tap buys the next step, the button shows the exact next-step cost, the line under it shows the new time, pips count 0 to 3 steps, a disabled state says why, and the top state reads At its fastest.

The engine is already built: processSpeed.ts canBuyOvertime / buyOvertime. Each step adds +25% of the normal rate, at most 3 steps (1.75x, never instant). Step k costs ceil(10 x the job's full minutes x its tier x k). The minutes are the job's whole length (durationTicks), never the time left, so a step costs the same whenever it is bought, and the price is fixed for that job. Buying rescales the time left; the job's size and its Fleet Admiral XP do not change.

Every sample number below is the engine's rule on real catalog data at the default 1 second tick, with no facility or Museum speed bonus. Every number in the build would come from the engine's quote, never from the UI.

Open calls, in one list

  1. One tap or a confirm: A one tap buys, as the gear card (drawn) or B a small Buy / Keep as is confirm for these jobs, since facility steps reach thousands of credits.
  2. Where the strip shows: A on every running job's progress row, always open (drawn) or B collapsed behind a small "Overtime" link that opens it.
  3. The bar marker: A a glyph and "1.25x" chip on the readout line (drawn) or B a brighter, hatched bar fill with the rate in the readout text.
  4. What the bar measures: A the Overtime rows switch to processWorkFraction, so a purchase never moves the bar (drawn) or B keep today's formula, so the bar jumps forward at each purchase.
  5. The completion log line: A "took 08:20 with Overtime (10:00 at the normal rate)" plus "Overtime: 2 of 3 steps", from one small engine field (drawn) or B no engine change, drop "Time elapsed" on sped-up jobs and show only the steps.
  6. Docks, Docking bays and the Systems Bay: A give those text-only "Upgrade already in progress" lines a slim bar, time and strip (drawn) or B leave them as text, with no Overtime offered there.
  7. Command Home In progress: A rate chip only, no button, the rows stay one tap to the console (drawn) or B a small sibling Overtime button beside each eligible row.
  8. Time format on the strip: A each strip uses its host row's clock ("04:48", "~3m 12s", "12 min 40 s") (drawn) or B "about 5 min" everywhere, as on the gear card.
  9. Emoji on rows this slice touches: A swap them for SVG glyphs while there: the damaged banner's wrench (ShipSystemsPanel.svelte), and Command Home's repair and facility icons, which fall back to emoji (App.svelte HOME_ICON_GLYPH) (drawn with stand-in glyphs) or B leave them for a separate pass.

Unchanged by this slice: the engine's prices, steps and refusals; no Overtime on refining, crafting, salvage, relic study, missions, patrols or expeditions; no cancel on a build; nothing ever prompts for Overtime.
