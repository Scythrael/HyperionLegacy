Player-ordered field repair (OC-F14)

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, Wiring WR-10, open call OC-F14, for your answer and sign-off before any build. The engine path exists and is tested; no screen offers it (registry row away:audit:fieldRepairOrder, pending). The first question is still whether to have it at all; if yes, pick a layout below.

Example run on every frame: the ship's frozen hull pool is 400, it carries 48 hull damage (a failed debris check, 16, plus a lost probe fight, 32), no Engineering repair speed and no Damage Control Suite. System, body and team names are samples. Every time shown is the engine's formula on those numbers; a build would read them from the engine, never from the UI.

0. What the engine does today (confirmed in code)

PieceWhereWhat it does
The orderexpedition.ts:3141 canOrderFieldRepair, :3149 orderFieldRepairAdvances the run to now, checks it, stores a repair leg starting now, or when the ship is back from a leg already under way (a pickup), then settles. A refusal changes nothing. Free: no credits, no materials.
RefusalsgroundOps.ts:2719 fieldRepairBlock, :2729 its wordsNo damage: "The ship has no damage to repair". A repair already stored and not finished: "The ship is already setting down for repairs". Ground operations over (or recalled): "The ground operations are over: the Shipyard will repair the ship at home". A run from before GO-8b: "This expedition launched before field repairs". Plus: not a Ground Operations run (no strip at all).
Where it sets downgroundOps.ts:2699 fieldRepairSite, :2672The nearest Terrestrial, Ocean, Arid or Frozen body to the body being scanned (the last body once the scan is done; ties go to the lower one). None in the system: it repairs in orbit where it is. The player does not pick the site.
How longgroundOps.ts:2712, model.ts:1903, groundOps.ts:451The Shipyard's time (20 s + 0.5 s per hull point, shortened by repair speed and the Damage Control Suite) x2 on the ground, x3 in orbit, plus 5m each way when the site is another body. Example, 48 damage: 1m 28s on the ground, 2m 12s in orbit.
What it fixesgroundOps.ts:2766, :2684All the hull damage the run carries at the order. Damage taken after the order stays for a later repair.
Costs and gainsgroundOps.ts:2656 to 2667, gearWear.ts:147, shipScanEvents.ts:243The scan waits for the whole leg; the ground teams keep working; a team pickup waits until the repair is done. While set down, the gear aboard (the ship's and its members') recovers at the field rate (40, vs 2 underway). Later ship battles this run start with the repaired hull. Without it, damage left at claim goes to the Shipyard, and a damaged ship cannot launch another expedition until repaired.
Automatic repairgroundOps.ts:2774, expedition.ts:2223Unchanged: at 25% of the hull pool (100 of 400 here) the ship sets down on its own, the same leg marked "after heavy damage". The order is the same leg earlier, marked "ordered".
RecallgroundOps.ts:1244Recalling during a repair stops it; the hull comes home with its damage.
Not in the engineCancelling an ordered repair, a partial repair, choosing the site. Not drawn below; see the questions.
On screen todayGroundTeamTimeline.svelte, groundOpsTimelineDisplay.ts:495, 710The Ship lane already draws a repair leg (the "field repair" segment, shipyard glyph) and its words. The run's hull damage is not shown anywhere during a run; both layouts add a hull readout.

4. Your calls

  1. OC-F14 itself: have it? Proposal: build the control (the hull readout and the order). Alternative: delete the engine path (orderFieldRepair, the "ordered" mark) and keep only the automatic repair at 25%.
  2. Layout: A (strip on the Ship lane, one tap) or B (header button beside Recall, with a confirm)? Drawn both; A is the Overtime strip's pattern, B is the Recall pattern.
  3. Confirm or not? With A, the preview is on screen before the tap; the order cannot be cancelled once given (the engine has no cancel; only Recall stops it). One tap, or one tap plus a confirm?
  4. The hull readout is new (no screen shows a run's hull damage today). Show it on every Ground Operations run, or only once the ship has taken damage?
  5. Engine question, flagged, not decided: when the scanned body is not a repair world, the engine always flies to the nearest one, even when repairing in orbit would be quicker. Example: 48 damage is 11m 28s with the trip (5m each way) against 2m 12s in orbit. Keep as built, or have the order take the quicker of the two? (An engine change; the automatic repair would follow the same rule.)
  6. A cancel? Not in the engine. Leave it out (the order is short and free), or add a cancel before the ship arrives? (New engine work.)
