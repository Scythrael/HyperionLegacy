Notable crew ticker in the Personnel header

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, for sign-off before the build. The user's calls: 2026-10-16 "Personnel bucket header (Recommended)", a slow, quiet ticker at the top of Personnel; 2026-11-02 "Add a slow auto-scroll", drifts gently, pauses on hover or touch, stays still with Reduce motion on; 2026-11-12 "Build the header ticker (Recommended)", one fleet-wide ticker at the top of Personnel, unassigned notables included, mockup first.

What was built instead so far: the per-ship Notable Crew strip on Ships, a ship, Complement (ComplementPanel.svelte .comp-nstrip), drifting by ui/marquee.ts. This mockup reuses that strip's content and its motion. The bottom nav calls this bucket Crew; its tabs are Admiral, Captain Roster and Refugees.

Everything below moves live. Hover an entry to pause; tap or click one to open its record. The switch previews the game's Reduce motion setting (Settings; it starts out matching the device's own reduced-motion setting, so it starts on here if your device asks for less motion).

Notable crew have no titles or milestones in the engine (identity only, user 2026-10-16). Titles and milestones belong to captains (Hall of Command, CP-16), so the ticker does not show any. Notables give no stat edge, so the ticker shows no numbers beyond the count.

2. The motion: option A (the strip drifts) and option B (one at a time)

A · Drift (desktop band). The Complement strip's own motion, ui/marquee.ts as built

Drifts to the end at 14 px a second, rests 1.8 s, drifts back, rests, and so on (it moves the strip's own scroll, nothing is cloned, so there is no wraparound seam). Pauses on hover, on keyboard focus inside it, and on touch (resumes 1.5 s after the finger lifts), so a tap never lands on a moving target. Swipe or wheel to scroll by hand; the drift carries on from there. Does not run while the tab is hidden, while the band is offscreen, or when everything fits. Reduce motion on: never moves; it is a plain row you scroll by hand, with the All list for the rest.

B · One at a time (desktop band). The Home board's "Needs your orders" cadence: a new entry every 3.2 s, fading

Shows one entry, fades to the next every 3.2 s (NEEDS_TICKER_INTERVAL_MS, the Home board's ticker), wraps around. The arrows step by hand at any time. Pauses on hover, focus or touch (a small "paused" tag shows). Reduce motion on: no fade and no auto-advance; it holds on one entry and the arrows step. With 12 notables a full cycle takes 38 s; with 50, 2 min 40 s.

3. Tap or click an entry: the notable's record, and the All list

Record, assigned (tap "Sennei Threnaal")

Record, unassigned (tap "Kirra Nirriss")

All (the band's All button): everyone, stationary

The record is a small read-only card in the game's ActionModal (Escape closes, focus returns to the band). Its star is the existing favorite toggle. Open Complement goes to Ships, that ship, the Complement tab, where its card already offers Send back and Retire. An unassigned notable has no ship to open; today a Personnel roster that pins it is the only way to seat it (an "assign to ship" button was logged for later on 2026-11-12), so the card says so. The All list is the same idiom as the Home board's "Show all": no motion at all, grouped by ship, Unassigned last; each row opens the record.
