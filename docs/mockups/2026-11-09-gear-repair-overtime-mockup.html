Gear repair: Overtime

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, gear wear step 5 follow-up (OT-UI, gear repair only), for sign-off before the build. The engine is already built (processSpeed.ts buyGearRepairOvertime): each Overtime step adds +25% of the normal repair rate, at most 3 steps (1.75x, never instant). Step k costs ceil(10 x job minutes x hull tier x k) credits, where the job is the ship's slowest worn piece at the normal rate, frozen when step 1 is bought. It works only on a docked ship (at home or in a Shipyard bay) and clears when the gear is full or the ship leaves. It is per ship in the engine: one purchase speeds every worn piece on that ship.

Sample numbers are the engine's real rule on a sample ship: Kestrel, a Destroyer (hull tier 2), docked at home (4% a minute). Its most worn piece is Hull Plating at 40%, so the job is 15 min: steps cost 300 / 600 / 900 cr and bring the whole ship's time to full from about 15 min to about 12, 10, then 9 min. The Railgun shown is at 70% (about 8 min alone). Repair itself stays free and automatic; Overtime is a quiet, optional extra and nothing ever asks for it.
