CP-9: Complement table, Graduate, Manifest

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, Tasks 25 and 26 UI part 1, for sign-off. The Complement sub-tab of a ship keeps its capacity header and division charts, and gains a Crew groups table under them (every buff shown, sort, division filter, row checkboxes), a Graduate one confirm, and a Manifest of generated names. Sample ship: Vesper Reach, an Aurora (base 750), manual crew, Elite Quarters installed (no added berths), so it sits 18 over cap after an Efficient Quarters swap. Never evict (OC-6): it just takes no more crew. Amber dashed boxes are open calls; the drawn option is marked.

  2. Buffs in a row: A one chip per buff (drawn) or B one comma line.
  3. Graduate placement: A a button per row (drawn) or B one button in the selection bar for a single ticked group.
  4. Phone filter rows: A two sideways-scrolling chip rows (drawn) or B a "Sort and filter" sheet.
  5. Phone selection bar: A sticky at the bottom (drawn) or B inline.
  6. Send back: A on each notable card here, or B only in Personnel Rosters. (Not in the CP-9 file list; say if you want it now.)
  7. Manifest layout: A one paged list (drawn) or B collapsed groups.
