Telescope map: enemy territory

Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0, Expedition Tiers G2-7 ("G2-7 OWNS shading the sectors on the telescope map"), for sign-off before the build. What enemy territory is, from the notes and the code: the user's G8 answer (2026-10-16) chose "Fixed sectors on the map", not a per-system chance. G2-1 built them in galaxy.ts: each homeworld has one fixed sector per Tier II to VII (Tier I has none), placed from the homeworld's own seed. A sector is that tier's band of distances across a wedge of bearings from the homeworld, its width the tier's share of a full turn (ENEMY_SECTOR_WIDTH_PERMILLE: II 5%, III 15%, IV 25%, V 35%, VI 45%, VII 50%, the layout still a [PROPOSAL]). G2-2 flags a candidate system as enemy territory exactly when its bearing lies in its tier's sector (inEnemySector), which raises its combat risk by one step.

The map is centered on the homeworld on a square-root scale, so each band is a ring around the homeworld and each sector is a slice of its ring: it maps onto the existing map with no distortion. Every shape below is the real geometry from telescopeMapView and homeworldPlacement for one sample save (homeworld 16 ly from Sol), at Subspace Telescope Level 6 (reach Tiers I to V; Level 7 dashed). Perseus (III) and Sagittarius (IV) share one band, so their two sectors share one ring.

Every save draws its own; the build reads stateHomeworld(state).enemySectors and telescopeMapRingRadius, adds one wedge path per sector in range to telescopeMapView, and tests that a candidate flagged enemy territory always lands inside a drawn slice. The dispatch console's small region map (G2-6) could reuse the same slices; not drawn here.

Open calls, in one list

  1. Shading style: A thin hatch with a dashed edge in the danger hue at low strength (drawn) or B a flat faint tint.
  2. Legend line: A shown only once a sector is in range (drawn) or B always, with "No enemy territory in range yet."
  3. Label wording: A "Enemy territory" with "part enemy" on region rows (drawn) or B "Known enemy territory" everywhere.
  4. Sectors beyond range: A hidden until the telescope reaches the tier (drawn) or B a faint dotted outline.

Peace rule: no red fill, no pulse, no warning glyph on the map, and nothing here blocks a launch; it only shows where the console's existing "Enemy territory" flag comes from.
