**Hyperion Legacy 0.13.11.4** is live.

Coming back after a long time away no longer freezes the game. With several patrols on Repeat, catching up could lock the page for many seconds, and for minutes on a phone. It is now much faster, and a long catch-up shows a progress bar instead. Your fleet earns exactly what it did before, and your save carries over untouched.

Full patch notes: <https://crystalisoft.com/game/hl/patch-notes>
