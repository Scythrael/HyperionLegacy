🛠️ **Hyperion Legacy 0.13.10** is live.

A quick fix: your fleet now keeps working at full pace while the game sits in a background tab or your device sleeps. Before, that time crawled by and was never made up. Now every second away counts, and after a long absence you get the same "While you were away" summary you see when reopening the game. Your save carries over untouched.
