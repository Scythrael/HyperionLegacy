🚀 **Hyperion Legacy 0.14.0: Crew and Exploration** is live.

Your ships get crews: a bridge of named officers, hundreds of crew aboard, and away teams that go down to the surface. And they finally have somewhere to go. Build an explorer and fly expeditions across seven regions of the galaxy, from the Local Bubble out to the Galactic Core: scan new worlds, study gas giants from orbit, and bring home relics of cultures long gone for your new Museum. Closer to home, Short-Range Missions run job after job and never fail, and Evacuations bring refugees home to work at your facilities.

Also in this one: training runs that cannot be lost, gear that wears gently and repairs itself for free, optional Overtime, a power budget and modules for every ship, material quality that carries into what you craft, and Homeworld and captain talents you already own that now do what they say. Your save migrates automatically and carries over.
