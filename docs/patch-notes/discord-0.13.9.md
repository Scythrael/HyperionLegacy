🛠️ **Hyperion Legacy 0.13.9** is live.

A round of fixes, many of them found by an outside play-through. Gear resting in Armory loadouts no longer eats your Systems Bay space, Swap now replaces an installed weapon or drone pod in one step, and salvaging small-recipe gear like the Plasma Cannon actually gives materials back. The retired Fuel Depot has stopped quietly using up your Deuterium Ice, ship range now counts your installed FTL Drive, and ships of the same type are numbered so you can tell them apart. Your save carries over untouched.
