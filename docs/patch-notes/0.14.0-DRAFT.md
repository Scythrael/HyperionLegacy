# 0.14.0 [DRAFT]

**version:** 0.14.0
**name:** Crew and Exploration [PLACEHOLDER, the name is yours]

**lede:** The biggest update yet. Your ships get crews: a bridge of named officers, hundreds of crew aboard, and away teams that go down to the surface. And they finally have somewhere to go. Explorer hulls fly expeditions across seven regions of the galaxy, from the Local Bubble out to the Galactic Core, scanning worlds, studying gas giants and bringing home relics of cultures long gone. Closer to home, Short-Range Missions run job after job, and Evacuations bring refugees home to work at your facilities. Along the way: training runs that cannot fail, gear that wears gently and repairs itself for free, Overtime for when you want something sooner, a power budget and modules for every ship, material quality that carries into what you craft, and talents you already own that now do what they say. Everything new keeps running while you are away, exactly as if you had watched. Your save migrates automatically and carries over.

## Feature: Crew Comes Aboard (isNew)

Lead: Every ship now has a bridge and a crew.

- **Senior Staff.** A ship's Senior Staff tab has 7 bridge seats: Executive Officer, Pilot, Operations, Science, Tactical, Medical and Engineering. Hiring is free. Each officer brings two bonuses, four stats that shape the department they head, and their origin's racial, and they rise from Cadet to Commander by meeting the objectives on their card.
- **The Complement.** Every hull has its own crew berths (an Eir holds 900, an Orion or Aurora 750, a Battleship or Carrier 160, a Destroyer 120) across six divisions: Security, Engineering, Science, Medical, Operations and Flight Deck. Every crew member adds a small buff of their own. A crew transport arrives every 30 minutes of game time with 12 new crew, and Auto-crew ships fill themselves from the pool.
- **Origins.** Crew and officers come from six human origins and four allied species, each with a small racial bonus, stronger on an officer.
- **Notable crew and the Manifest.** Graduate any crew member into a named notable (up to 50 in the fleet), shown in a gentle band under the Crew tab. The Manifest lists everyone aboard by name, a page at a time.
- **A free bridge, quarters and sensors.** [NEW DRAFT LINE 2026-11-17, for your review] Every ship comes with a free Standard-Issue Sensor Suite (Balanced Array), Bridge Module (Balanced Command: every seated officer works 10% better) and Crew Quarters (Utilitarian Quarters: 12 berths, every division works 10% better). The Quartermaster issues a free replacement of each, and any crafted module is an upgrade.
- **Nobody is ever lost.** Crew are never evicted when berths shrink, and salvaging a hull sends its crew back to the pool, its officers to Available Officers and its away team to Unassigned. Crew that no berth can take wait safely in the new Homeworld reserve.

## Feature: Personnel Rosters (isNew)

Lead: Save a ship's people once, apply them anywhere.

- **Saved rosters.** The new Personnel Rosters facility saves a ship's officers and notable crew as a named roster, optionally linked to an Armory loadout and squad loadouts. Apply it from the facility or from the roster picker on a ship's Senior Staff and Complement tabs; nothing changes until you press Apply.
- **The Crew Pool.** Its own tab lists every crew member waiting for a ship, per division, and sends them to a manual ship. Transfer moves crew between ships from a ship's Complement tab.
- **Bigger candidate boards.** The officer board and the away-team recruit board each show 10 candidates. Five upgrade levels (3,000 to 15,000 credits) add 2 to each board, up to 20.

## Feature: Retirement and the Hall of Command (isNew)

Lead: A captain's career can now end in honor, and be remembered.

- **Retire a captain.** A captain at level 25 or higher can retire from the Prestige tab. Their Executive Officer takes the captaincy, the slot starts again at level 1 with a prestige point for a second talent tree, and every retirement adds 5% to the captain XP your whole fleet earns. Each slot can retire 5 times, and the confirm warns you before the last one.
- **The Hall of Command.** Every retirement writes a card in the Archive: the level, the missions flown, the in-game day and the captain's notable firsts.
- **Levels never stop.** There is no top captain level. Past level 30 each level costs 8% more experience than the last, compounding, so the climb slows gently the higher a captain goes.

## Feature: Explorer Hulls and Sensors (isNew)

Lead: Three new hulls built to go far, and a new stat that lets them see.

- **The Orion, Aurora and Eir.** Research each hull's blueprint at the Research Lab, then build it at the Shipyard. Explorers carry enormous shields and reach, a frail hull and almost no guns, and they are the only hulls that can fly an expedition.
- **Sensors.** A new ship stat. Explorers carry their own (Aurora 35, Orion 25, Eir 15) and warships a little (10). A Sensor Suite adds to it, and the Science crew and Science officer multiply it.
- **Bridge and quarters modules.** Ship Systems gains Bridge Module and Crew Quarters tiles beside the Sensor Suite. Every Bridge Module, Crew Quarters module and the early sensor arrays can be researched and crafted with no relic.
- **Hull bonuses.** Each Bridge Module and Crew Quarters module also gives its hull family's bonus (for example +10% sensors on an explorer, +5% weapon damage on a warship), shown on the module's tile. Move it to another hull family and the bonus changes with it.

## Feature: Expeditions (isNew)

Lead: Seven regions of the galaxy, and a homeworld of your own to set out from.

- **Your homeworld.** Every save, yours included, places its homeworld at its own spot in the Local Bubble, so your distances and your galaxy map are yours alone.
- **Seven tiers.** The expedition card (Ops, Exploration) has a tab for each region, Tier I to Tier VII, each offering 3 candidate systems. Deeper tiers hold more worlds, pay more (up to x3.3 at Tier VII) and carry more risk, and from Tier II out each region holds a sector of enemy territory.
- **Drive classes and reach.** A ship needs a drive class at least the tier's number (Class I to Class VII, from its installed FTL Drive) and, from Tier III, enough sensors to chart the region. The tab names the first thing a ship is missing, and a system beyond reach says how far it is and how far the ship can go.
- **Scanning and scan events.** The ship scans each body from orbit while debris, probes, sensor ghosts and storms keep things interesting. Light hull damage adds up and the ship repairs itself in the field, or you can order a field repair yourself with one tap whenever the hull is damaged.
- **Recall and Resume.** Recall a run with one confirm. A system you leave with work still to do waits in its region's Resume list, with every finished scan and every objective's progress kept.

## Feature: Away Teams and Ground Operations (isNew)

Lead: Security fights, Science does the fieldwork, and nobody's progress is ever thrown away.

- **Squads and teams.** Every ship carries its own ground crew in Security and Science squads, paired into teams (Ground, Strike or Survey). Hire members free from the recruitment board on a ship's Away Team tab, and install Armor, a Weapon and a Tool on each.
- **Objectives.** When a body's scan finishes, its objectives appear already assigned by the default plan. Science moves the progress bar, and at each fight point Security must win a fight to go on. A lost fight keeps the progress, and the team comes back after the medical bay.
- **Reading the odds.** Each objective shows its difficulty (Simple to Deadly, harder ones paying more) and odds chips from Guaranteed Victory to Impossible. Systems are rated on four risk factors, objectives can carry affixes such as Reinforcing, and tougher objectives end with an Elite or a Boss.
- **Tactical Protocols.** Each Security member fights on their own Tactical Protocols (Ops, Exploration), with ready presets. Set one member's protocols as the default for new hires with Set for new hires.
- **Implants.** Members have implant slots (2 for Martian and Stheni members). The unique Neural Backup can revive its wearer once per battle.

## Feature: Field Kits (isNew)

Lead: Supplies for the fights your away teams meet.

- **Six kits.** First Aid Kit, Antitoxin, Combat Stim, Splint, Trauma Kit and Injury Kit. Research and craft them, or buy them on the Quartermaster's new Purchase tab.
- **Packs and the free set.** Every Security member packs their own kits (one of each by default) and lands on every objective with a free set that refills each time. Turn on the Quartermaster top-up and a launch buys only what the packs are short. Unused kits come home.
- **Grenades and field devices.** Stun, Toxin and Impact Grenades install in the Tool slot with charges that reset every landing. The Shroud Emitter makes ambushes less likely, and the Lure Beacon makes them more likely in return for richer salvage.
- **Conditions and the medical bay.** Enemies can leave Bleed, Concussion or a Fracture that worsens to a Broken or Shattered Bone. The right kit clears it in the fight, or the medical bay mends it on the trip back.

## Feature: The Subspace Telescope (isNew)

Lead: See what lies ahead before you launch.

- **Detail by level.** A homeworld facility that reads a system before you go: from which risks are present, up through each world's grades, a recommended team composition with a button to set it, and a full forecast of the hardest fight. Strong ship sensors lift the reading one level.
- **The galaxy map.** Centered on your homeworld, with each region's badge, your range, hatched enemy territory and a green arc for how much of each region you have completed.
- **Blind launches.** A system beyond the telescope's range can still be visited. It just asks first.

## Feature: Gas Giants, Nebulae and the Survey Log (isNew)

Lead: Some worlds are studied from orbit.

- **Ship studies.** A gas giant has no surface, so the ship studies it from orbit instead, and some systems hold a nebula to sweep. No squad is needed. Science crew and a Galileo-Class Probe make a study faster and richer.
- **Gas Storage Canisters.** Studies fill canisters of hydrogen, methane, ammonia, deuterium, helium-3 and the rare antimatter cell, each with its own uses, and every canister sells at the Quartermaster.
- **Research data.** Studies bring home research data in eight families. The Research Lab's new Data study lane studies it on its own and banks boosts that speed up research in that family (+12% for one).
- **Ancient data.** Now and then a study recovers an Ancient Data Fragment. Decode enough entries of one culture to open three special blueprints: Pressure-Forged Plating, the Ramscoop Collector and the Resonance Sensor Array.
- **The Survey Log.** A new Archive tab records every giant and nebula you have read: each giant's class and mass, and each nebula's place in the life of stars. One place in the galaxy leaves an entry of its own.

## Feature: Relics and the Museum (isNew)

Lead: The galaxy is full of what came before.

- **Relic blanks.** Away teams recover relics on expeditions (short-range missions turn one up now and then too). Each arrives as an unidentified blank with a region tier and a rarity.
- **Identification.** Queue blanks on the Research Lab's Identification tab. It is free, runs on its own lanes that grow with the Lab, and keeps going while you are away.
- **Exceptional relics.** A rare few are named pieces from fourteen exceptional sets, Tier I to Tier VII. Each piece adds a bonus while you hold it, and a complete set adds a big one.
- **Cultures.** Every relic belongs to a long-dead culture. Study 10 of their finds and you learn their name on the Research Lab's Cultures tab.
- **The Museum.** A new Archive tab shelves every relic by region tier and rarity. Each copy adds to its bonus, with a soft cap at 1,000 relics per tier.

## Feature: System Completion (isNew)

Lead: Every system you visit keeps a score, and nothing is lost for leaving one unfinished.

- **A completion % per world and per system.** The Expedition Console shows it with a small ring, and a skipped objective still counts as done, so 100% is always reachable.
- **The planet bonus.** Each planet that reaches 100% earns one bonus relic blank, one rarity step up, once per planet.
- **The Systems list.** The Survey Log lists every system you visit from this update on, at its best %, and a finished system wears the Completed badge.

## Feature: Short-Range Missions (isNew)

Lead: Set-and-forget jobs close to home that can never fail.

- **A new Ops tab, Short-Range.** Send a captain to a board slot and it runs job after job, a new one every cycle: Refugee Relocation, Evacuation, Supply Drop-off and Pickup, Phenomenon Survey, Telescope Repair, Comm Booster Build and Prisoner Transport.
- **One requirement sheet per tier.** Range, cargo, guest quarters, sensors, and Engineering and Security crew ratings. Meet it once and the ship can take any job the slot draws. The board pre-picks your best ready captain, and Send dispatches in one tap.
- **More slots.** Every save starts with two Tier I slots, and Mission Control upgrades open Tier II slots.
- **Guest quarters.** Ships now carry passengers in guest quarters (the Eir, Orion, Aurora and General Freighter have them, and the new Guest Wing adds more), never in the cargo hold.
- **Calm by design.** Repeat is on by default. A pickup with a full Warehouse or a drop-off short of credits simply waits at home and leaves when it can, and nothing is lost while it waits.

## Feature: Refugees (isNew)

Lead: Evacuations bring people home, and they make your facilities better.

- **Named people.** Each Evacuation can bring one named refugee home, with a background and a skill (harder evacuations bring more capable people), and your first one always does.
- **Posts.** Every facility opens posts as it levels. Each background matches one facility for a strong buff (a Scholar at the Research Lab, a Refiner at the Refinery, a Fuel handler at the Fuel Depot, and more), and anyone helps a little anywhere.
- **Moving is free, any time.** No cost, no timer. Everyone not posted waits on the new Refugees tab under Crew, and nobody posted ever leaves.
- **Special arrivals.** Now and then, someone special comes home. You will know them when you see them.

## Feature: Training Runs (isNew)

Lead: Your first run of each kind is a gentle one.

- **First runs cannot be lost.** Your first expedition from an explorer and your first patrol are training runs: they always succeed, and your gear takes no wear. The first job of each short-range type is its smallest, and a gathering mission can never be lost.
- **A gentle Tier I.** After the training run, Tier I risk rises slowly with each run you complete, reaching full strength after 10. From Tier II on, every run is at full strength: the tier is the step.
- **Already a veteran?** A save that has already flown patrols stays at full strength for them, exactly as before.

## Feature: Gear Wear and Free Repair (isNew)

Lead: Gear wears gently with use and recovers on its own. Repair is always free.

- **Condition.** Every piece of gear wears in its own way (cargo bays by hauls, drives by distance, weapons by battle) and shows its condition: Pristine, Sound, Worn or Battered. Ordinary use stops at 60%, and even at its lowest a piece keeps at least 75% of its stats.
- **Nothing breaks.** Wear never breaks, salvages or uninstalls anything, and it never blocks a run.
- **Repair is automatic and free.** Gear recovers 4% a minute at home, faster in a Shipyard repair bay. Command Home shows a quiet Fleet care section while anything is recovering. It only reports, and nothing on it asks for a click.

## Feature: Overtime (isNew)

Lead: Pay a little to finish something sooner. Always optional.

- **Up to three steps.** Each step speeds a running job by 25% of its normal rate, up to 1.75x. Never double, never instant.
- **Where.** Hull builds and repairs, research, data studies, facility upgrades, the Docks, docking bays and the Systems Bay, plus gear repair on a docked ship. One tap buys a step, and the button shows its exact price.
- **Nothing else changes.** The bar does not jump, it just fills faster, and the job delivers exactly what it would have.

## Feature: Power and Modules (isNew)

Lead: Every ship now has a power budget, and new accessories to spend it on.

- **The Power bar.** Ship Systems shows each ship's power output against the draw of everything installed. Every loadout you already have stays within its budget, and nothing is ever switched off: a ship over budget just fights a little weaker until you rebalance.
- **Modules.** Small accessories you research (from Tier II), craft and install in a ship's Modules row: Passive, Proc, Conditional, Active and paired Set modules, plus Specials that come from discoveries, like the Precursor Lens.
- **Reactor Cores.** Power a crafted core leaves unused now speeds the ship's drive, and a crafted core never makes a ship slower than the Standard-Issue one.

## Feature: Material Lines and Quality (isNew)

Lead: Recipes that make physical sense, and quality that carries through.

- **Five material lines.** The Refinery's Production tab groups every recipe by where its raws come from: Structural, Heavy metals, Recovered tech, Organic and Volatiles. Two new refined goods join them: Biofiber and Resin Distillate.
- **Recipes now make physical sense.** Ablative plating and away-team armor are woven from Biofiber, seals and toxin grenades use Resin Distillate, and many ship systems now use material-line goods. Salvaging a piece gives back parts of its current recipe.
- **Quality carries through.** An order's output starts from the average quality of what it used. Turn on Use best materials on an order to spend your highest quality stock first.
- **Quality blanks.** Crafted gear now keeps the quality of its materials: each blank holds the average quality its craft used, and inspecting takes your highest quality blank first, starting the piece from that quality.
- **Material Quality works.** Refinery-Feed Rigs (and every Prospecting Rig's Material Quality line) now give each unit a mission delivers a chance to arrive one quality higher.
- **Deuterium has a job again.** The new Cometary Ice Harvest (Tier I) brings home Deuterium Ice, and the Refinery presses 10 into a Deuterium Canister. Reactor Core blueprints and FTL Drives from Class III up are crafted with canisters.

## Feature: New Missions and the Fuel Depot (isNew)

Lead: More ways to put a ship to work, and a reason to build the Fuel Depot again.

- **Survey missions.** Survey Mineral Anomaly and Survey Stellar Phenomena bring home rarer finds, more with stronger sensors.
- **Supply runs.** The Frontier Supply Run and Colony Supply Lift spend credits on supplies and are paid back with a margin on delivery.
- **The Fuel Depot extends reach.** Every level adds 5% to the reach of every ship in the fleet, for missions, patrols and expeditions alike. Levels you built before still count.

## Feature: Combat: Stances, Torpedoes and the Flight Deck

Lead: Fights open at range and close, and where a ship holds now matters.

- **Three stances.** Aggressive closes until every gun fires. Balanced holds where most of its firepower reaches. Defensive (formerly Standoff) holds at its longest gun's reach, takes less damage and deals a little less, puts its shields first and turns its point defense and drones on enemy drones first. A ship closing in before its guns reach is harder to hit.
- **Torpedoes.** Some pirates now carry a Concussion Torpedo: every Corsair Carrier, and a Marauder more often as your patrols go on (up to 40%). A ship flying Defensive with a working Point-Defense Array or EMP Cannon shoots some of them down. The patrol card's More info and the Threat Assessment both count them.
- **The Flight Deck rallies.** On a carrier patrol below half hull, the Flight Deck's Integrity helps its crew rebuild lost drones faster.

## Feature: Talents That Now Work

Lead: If you own these, they now do exactly what they say.

- **Works Directorate.** Each Fabricator job has a 5% chance to deliver one more of its output, decided when the job starts.
- **Combat Readiness.** The Tactician hub: +5% weapon damage on the ship its captain commands.
- **Survey Doctrine.** The Explorer hub: +5% expedition scan rate.
- **The Tactician and Explorer specializations.** Tactician gives +1% damage from the ship's weapons (not its drones); Explorer gives +1% relic find chance.
- **Home Guard.** +5% shield capacity and recharge on patrols.
- **Civic Charter.** Every crew transport brings 1 more crew.
- **Salvage Operations.** Its yield bonus now also applies when you salvage a whole ship: 10 percentage points more of the hull's components and credits come back.
- **Your captain's origin.** The captain in command now carries their origin's racial bonus too, at an officer's size.

## Category: Heads Up for Current Players (tone: balance)

- **Your ships got their crew modules.** [NEW DRAFT LINE 2026-11-17, for your review] Every ship you already had was given the free Sensor Suite, Bridge Module and Crew Quarters, once, in any of those slots that was empty. A module you installed yourself is never replaced, and a slot you empty afterwards stays empty. A ship out on a run gets its set too and finishes the run exactly as it started.
- **Archive completion reads lower.** The Archive gained slots for all the new gear (away-team gear, crew modules, sensor arrays, modules and more), so your completion percentage drops. Nothing you enshrined is lost.
- **Standoff is now Defensive.** Your patrols keep their stance under its new name, and Defensive now does more (see Combat above).
- **The Warband is a tougher fight.** With fights opening at range and torpedoes in the mix, the Crimson-Reaver Warband's Threat Assessment may read a harder band for the same ship. Defensive with point defense helps.
- **Captains past level 30.** Their XP bar will look shorter, because the next level now costs more (a level 60 captain's next level goes from 18,000 to about 181,000 XP). No levels are lost and no XP is taken away. Nothing at or below level 30 changes.
- **Standing craft lines on changed recipes.** A continuous or batch line on a reworked recipe waits until you have the new materials, then carries on. A job already running finishes exactly as it started, and gear you already crafted keeps every stat.
- **Repeat runs and the new reactor speed.** A mission on Repeat that is already out when you update picks up a crafted Reactor Core's speed once you send it again. Patrols pick it up at their next cycle.
- **Reclaimed Alloy is now tagged uncommon** (it refines from common Scrap Alloy).
- **Old blanks.** Crafted blanks you already hold read as quality Q0.

## Category: Additional Improvements

- **Roman numerals.** Every tier and drive class now reads in Roman numerals, from Research to the Warehouse.
- **Graph colors.** Settings, UI, Accessibility, Graph colors offers Default, Color-blind safe and Monochrome for the crew rank bars.
- **The Quartermaster.** Its new Purchase tab sells field kits, its Sell tab now buys gas canisters and more, and its Requisition tab now also issues the free Sensor Suite, Bridge Module and Crew Quarters [this last part is a NEW DRAFT LINE 2026-11-17].
- **Help.** New topics for every new system, from Expedition Tiers to Refugees, with in-game links that land on the right page.

**save:** Your save migrates automatically and carries over: every ship, captain, system, upgrade and setting is preserved, and a run already underway finishes exactly as it would have. Your save is given its own homeworld. The save format advances from version 54 to 72 across this release.
