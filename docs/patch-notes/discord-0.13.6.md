🚀 **Hyperion Legacy 0.13.6: Item Lifecycle** is live.

We followed a crafted system through its whole life this release. It starts as a blank on the bench, gets rolled at the new Inspect table, joins a loadout in the Armory, and, if it is your best work, earns a place in the Archive. While we were at it, we finally put fuel out of its misery: it is a ship's range in lightyears now, not a chore.

Rounding it out: a Quartermaster you can sell your retired stock to, spares you can favorite and lock, a header currencies band you arrange yourself, four new accent themes, action windows that all behave one way, and a polish pass across the consoles. Your save carries over automatically.
