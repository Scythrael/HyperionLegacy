## The First Traversal — a campaign-ending hopelessness cliffhanger (user, 2026-09-16)

The moment DT is unlocked is NOT a triumphant upgrade screen. It is a **hopelessness cliffhanger that
ENDS one of the campaigns**, revealed only after everything has already been lost. Beats:

**The setup (hopeless, before DT is even hinted at):**
- The enemy is closing in. The homeworld is surrounded. The weapon that dispensed your doom has
  ALREADY delivered its deadly device, and it is turning the planet into ruins. There is no counter,
  no rescue. The player has run out of options on purpose.

**The cinematic (the campaign's final scene):**
- The Fleet Admiral turns their chair away from the console, the fight already lost.
- A brilliant flash: entire swaths of the homeworld city are turned to ash.
- One brilliant bolt hurtles toward the Admiral, and as it nears it begins to **slow down**.
- The blinding light engulfs the Admiral and their command building (name TBD, the eventual
  command-center location). Everything goes white.
- The Admiral awakens ALONE in a white void, and there discovers the mechanisms for Dimensional
  Traversal. The first traversal unlocks at the exact instant of annihilation.

## The mechanic the beat establishes
- **First DT unlocks at the moment of destruction.** From the white void, you can traverse back to
  just BEFORE that event, and dimensionally traverse FORWARD from that point.
- **Traversal = a knowledge-intact loop (New Game+ shaped).** Each traversal adds additional STORY
  points and you come back with your **knowledge intact**, and potentially with some of what you
  started with carried over, growing with you as your powers grow.
- **You bring more each time.** The first traversal lets you bring CAPTAINS with you; each subsequent
  traversal lets you bring more / accomplish more, because you are steadily growing more powerful and
  can carry more forward. This escalation IS part of the storyline and how it unfolds.
- Ties into the prestige/scaling architecture (multiplicative-at-prestige) and the meta-achievement
  tier (DT-spanning milestones) already logged in SUGGESTIONS.

## Dimensional Traversal: the scaling + per-dimension system (from the 2026-08-21 design pass)

Ported here from memory [[project_fleet_admiral_first_cause_dt]] into the git-durable doc (the
original full prose lived in an ephemeral scratchpad and is gone; this is the recorded substance).
⚠️ If detail is missing versus what was originally typed, the user should flag it and we re-add it.

- **DT is the prestige BACKBONE and the STORY spine, not a number faucet.** A Torment-tier ladder,
  DT I..X.
- **Scaling difficulty:** number inflation is COSMETIC (both sides balloon, enemies ~x2 / player
  ~x1.75 per tier) with a small controlled NET delta (~10-25% harder per tier) that is the REAL
  difficulty. The design UNIT is WIN RATE (a fresh destroyer ~100% at DT0 -> ~85% at DT1), measurable
  with the existing combat-1.0 harness (resolvePatrolWaves / forecast / patrol-balance).
- **Each dimension is UNIQUE (the "new hazards / changes as you go from dimension to dimension"):**
  every DT is its own dimension with SYMMETRIC world-modifiers grounded in real physics (fine-tuned
  constants + matter/antimatter Big-Bang seeding). Each grants a prestige-tree point plus a powerful
  buff (buffs-as-VERBS, e.g. "5% duplicate-on-craft"), and GATES future systems (the 2.0 dreadnought /
  "FA operations" unlock around DT V).
- **Dimension-Shifted gear = the endgame loot chase** (a flat base +% is just seasoning).
- **Architecture:** ONE type-aware CENTRALIZED scaling seam (magnitudes vs probabilities scale
  differently, so +5% crit does not warp the crit meta); ADDITIVE so DT0 is today's game
  byte-identical (existing saves + the combat debut untouched until the first transit). Derived
  readouts (Battle Rating, Threat Assessment) are the HONESTY layer so cosmetic big numbers never mask
  the true net delta.
- Ties to the prestige/scaling architecture in SUGGESTIONS (additive-within-prestige,
  multiplicative-at-prestige) and the Safety-Interlocks endgame risk axis.

## Threat escalation: LEVIATHANS (user, 2026-09-16, a colleague's idea)
The First Cause / DBDs (name TBD) do not only field normal-sized troops. As the situation grows
progressively worse, they deploy **Kaiju / Leviathan-scale entities** as part of what they throw at
you: a visceral signal that the threat is massive and escalating. In gameplay these appear as random
boss encounters during exploration surveying (a cascading, prepared-only, escape-if-you-must away-team
fight); the full mechanic lives in the Exploration saga doc, Part D.1 (Away-Team combat). Lore-wise,
the Leviathans are one of the antagonist's escalation FORMS, sitting alongside the 5 escalating Modes
already recorded in memory [[project_fleet_admiral_first_cause_dt]].
