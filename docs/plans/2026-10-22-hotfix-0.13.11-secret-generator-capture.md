## Recon (verified in git)

- The original game is the 2026-07-02 prototype `66687f3` "closed-form generator stack, tier 1 prestige, versioned
  saves". MODULES: Mining Laser (Common Ore, base 1/s, cost 10, x1.15), Refinery (Refined Ingots, 0.4/s, 60, x1.17),
  Fabricator (Components, 0.12/s, 400, x1.2), Synthesizer (Alloys, 0.04/s, 2500, x1.22, gated by Alloy Synthesis
  research). Specializations (+25% per resource), a small skill tree, prestige. Retired 2026-07-06 (`ddf3cfa`, `f3a330b`,
  `e15a668`). Last full model before removal: `git show ddf3cfa^:src/lib/game/model.ts`.
- Current settings: the gear opens the System modal; its "Settings" tab (key `options`) has sub-tabs UI, Gameplay,
  Confirmations, Save Data (`OPTIONS_TABS` in App.svelte).
