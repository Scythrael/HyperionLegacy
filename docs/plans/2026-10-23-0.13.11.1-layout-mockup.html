6 · Help [?]: everything you need to know

THE STACK

Mining Lasers make Ore. Every higher generator makes the one below it: Refineries make Mining Lasers, Fabricators make Refineries, and so on up to the Singularity Engine. Ore buys everything.

THE TEN BONUS

Every 10 of a generator you buy doubles its output.

OVERCLOCK

Speeds up the whole stack by ×1.125 per level, and the levels multiply. Its price follows your income.

PRESTIGE

Captain Prestige resets your stack for Captain Points. Fleet Prestige resets that too, for Augment Points, a Skill Point and new generators. Prestigeception resets everything for Dream Points, which make every prestige pay more.

SKILLS, MILESTONES, TIME AWAY

Skills automate the clicking. Milestones are recorded for a future reward. The stack keeps running while you're away.

Plain-language help in one scrollable view [PROPOSAL wording]; this is where "makes Ore" and the rest of the rules now live.
