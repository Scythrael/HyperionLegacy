PATCH 0.13.5
Presentation
Draft · 2026‑09
Save-safe · 46→48

A top-to-bottom interface pass: interface scaling and contrast, a reorganized Settings screen, one unified item tooltip, redesigned Facilities / Captains / Operations consoles, and a new color-blind palette. No economy changes — your save migrates automatically and carries over untouched.

Accessibility & Scaling New

The interface can be tuned to how you read it.

  • Interface scaling. One setting scales the entire UI together, from 0.9× to 1.5×.
  • Contrast. Dim and disabled text was re-measured against WCAG AA on all six themes and raised where it fell short.
  • Color-blind palette. An opt-in Okabe-Ito palette remaps the status colors (success / warning / danger) and the rarity ladder; your theme's accent is unchanged.
  • Reduce motion, high contrast, and a dyslexia-friendly font live in Settings › UI › Accessibility.

Settings, Reorganized

Options are grouped by what you're trying to change.

  • Four sections — UI, Gameplay, Confirmations, Save Data — each its own panel.
  • Every explanation moved behind a tap / hover ? instead of standing text under each row.
  • On/off settings became switches; anything that picks from a list became a dropdown.
  • Confirmation levels. One dropdown sets every prompt at once (Ask everything → Stop asking). Edit any checkbox by hand and the level reads Custom; switching away from Custom asks before overwriting your edits.

The Item Tooltip, Unified

One tooltip for everything you can inspect.

  • Materials, gear, ships, ship systems, and in-progress crafts share a single tooltip: stats above the divider, flavor below.
  • Reflects rarity and quality. Weapons show full combat stats — damage range, projectiles, accuracy, fire rate, range, family.
  • In-progress crafts show the stat ranges and the affixes you can still roll into.
  • It now appears everywhere an item does: system tiles, the warehouse, salvage, combat, and tappable drop-table item names.

Consoles Redesigned

Facilities, Captains, and Operations read like the Home board.

  • Facilities and Captains are full-width panes with aligned status and progress rows.
  • Consoles use a single-row header: back · name · sub-tabs.
  • Captains: rename in-frame (no popup), a fleshed-out Overview, and Talents / Ship / Equipment tabs.
  • Operations: gathering and combat missions condensed into rows with More info + Assign / Dispatch; combat setup moved into a Dispatch popup with threat and stance explainers.

Auto-Salvage, Clearer & Safer

The rules moved to where you change how the game behaves.

  • Rules moved from the Salvage Bay to Settings › Gameplay, behind a master toggle.
  • Quality tiers and rarity bands are each multi-selects, and they narrow each other — only gear matching both is taken.
  • With nothing selected, nothing is ever taken.
  • Confirm-before-salvaging moved to the Confirmations tab.

The Header

Rebuilt and unified across phone and desktop.

  • FA EXP, CRAFT, and TICK share one aligned stat block — your crafting level now sits beside your Fleet Admiral level.
  • Settings has its own gear button; the portrait opens your menu.
  • Tap currency for a full breakdown; tap fuel to jump to the Fuel Depot.

Additional Improvements

  • Squared corners across the interface for one consistent shape.
  • Every progress bar fills smoothly instead of once per tick, combat replays included.
  • Recently Completed is one line per item, expandable for detail.
  • Idle-captain prompts route to the board that captain's ship can actually be dispatched from.
  • Mission dispatch timers show the ship-effective time, formatted as seconds / minutes / hours / days.
  • Specialty equipment (e.g. the Prospecting Rig) is gated by hull class only, not by captain specialization.

Bug Fixes

  • Reduce motion now makes every bar step, and the tick bar reaches 100% instead of vanishing at 90%.
  • The Settings ? help opens on the first tap on touch.
  • The Home in-progress row for refining / fabrication shows the whole order's remaining time, not a single item's.
  • Dispatch time no longer under-reports the true extraction time on prospecting missions.

Balance

  • Every newly built ship launches with a Standard-Issue weapon in every hardpoint — no empty slots.
  • The Crimson-Reaver patrols were retuned to hold their prior difficulty after that change.
  • Existing ships are unchanged; the Quartermaster issues free Standard-Issue weapons to fill any slot.
Save compatibility. Your save migrates automatically (schema 46 → 48) and carries over untouched.

Format notes: marquee features get a header + one-line lead + bullets; smaller changes fall under the flat category buckets (Improvements / Bug Fixes / Balance, tinted by bullet color). Tone is matter-of-fact. This same structure renders in-app (System › Patch Notes) and on the Landing page; older versions keep their existing prose.
