# 2.0 "Expansion": the three-pronged system (Prestige -> Flagship -> Active Mode)

Living design doc for the 2.0 major version / expansion, from the user's 2026-09-18 reveal to their
co-workers. This is the SINGLE consolidated home for the three-pronged system. It elaborates and, in
a few places, REFINES the earlier "ACTIVE PLAY / FLEET ADMIRAL FLAGSHIP" 2.0 entry in SUGGESTIONS.md
(~line 158) and ties to the prestige/story north-star in `docs/lore/first-cause-dimensional-traversal.md`
and memory [[project_fleet_admiral_first_cause_dt]]. US English, no em dashes.

Status: DEEP-FUTURE north-star, NOT scheduled. 2.0 is a full version / expansion release (its scope
is too large for a point patch). Everything here is design intent captured verbatim-faithfully; where
the user said a mechanism is not nailed down, it is marked LEANING / OPEN, not decided. Per the
standing rule: propose freely, decide never, and introduce no game entity the user did not name.

## Framing
- 2.0 is a **three-pronged system**, released together because the three prongs unlock one another in
  sequence and only make sense as a whole.
- The whole thing is **gated behind STORY progression**. As the player moves through story beats /
  chapters (count TBD), they reach a point where **System 1 unlocks**; using System 1 unlocks
  **System 2**; System 2's first loop unlocks **System 3**.
- Narrative spine: the Fleet Admiral is tired of sitting at the homeworld. He joined up to explore the
  stars with his circle of best friends. His command staff (old friends from before "the incident")
  keep telling him the days of exploration for him and his crew are long over, and for a while he
  believes them and misses those days. ("The incident" is introduced in 0.14.0, the story's intro.)

## System 1: PRESTIGE (unlocked by story progression)
- Reached through the storyline. The player eventually hits the beat where prestige is released. (The
  user withheld the in-fiction NAME of the system for the reveal, but it is prestige.)
- **Every prestige turns the game on its head in some fundamental way.** As the prestige system
  evolves, more and more carries forward into the next loop.
- Example the user gave (one of many possible per-loop shake-ups): a phenomenon sweeps across the
  galaxy and brings **periodic anomalies that can appear anywhere and at any time in space, adding new
  hazards to combat.** Framed explicitly as "just one of many many options."
- **Using System 1 unlocks System 2.**
- Ties: this is the same prestige backbone as the Dimensional Traversal / First Cause north-star (see
  the lore doc: DT is "the prestige BACKBONE and the STORY spine"), and it must obey the logged
  scaling architecture (additive-within-prestige, multiplicative-at-prestige; SUGGESTIONS ~line 1309).

## System 2: THE FLAGSHIP / "Dreadnought" (unlocked by the first prestige loop)
- **Narrative:** the first prestige loop is a restart, so the Admiral gets to do things differently.
  He says NOTHING of the plan or of "the prestige" to his crew, and gently paves the way to what he
  wanted from the start (now he has incentive). Only he knows what is going on so far. He coaxes his
  crew into agreeing with him: this time he will not sit around while the mystery is unsolved.
- **The Flagship:** the Admiral beams up to his personal Flagship; his SENIOR STAFF are the crew
  manning the seats, all with **bonuses exclusive to this mode**.
- The flagship class is the **Dreadnought** (named).
- It does **1 of 2 things**:
  - **PASSIVE mode:** an **11th ship**. (Captains go up to 10; the flagship is the 11th.) Instead of
    the fleet's 9-ish ship choices you get ONE, and it is a beast. "Another captain but better."
  - **ACTIVE mode:** the real kicker (that is System 3, below).
- **R&D gating (in-lore):** the dreadnought needs a lot of research and development, spread out
  through the FIRST prestige loop, and it must be done **in-lore** and gradually. Reason: if these
  capabilities appear out of nowhere it makes people suspicious, and the Admiral must prevent that
  (secrecy is load-bearing for the story). So the flagship's power is earned across the loop, not
  handed over at unlock.
- **Three dreadnought modes, mechanism OPEN (user "hasn't quite nailed down" how it will work).**
  Current LEANING: a single **prototype ship class that is heavily MODULAR**. To change role you go to
  the Shipyard and modules are swapped in, e.g. swap to a prospecting-style dreadnought and you become
  like one of the other 10 captains but souped up. Same for tactical and exploration. One-of-a-kind
  ship you keep noodling with via research and design.
  - ⚠️ CHANGE vs the earlier log: SUGGESTIONS ~line 159 recorded "THREE active modes = THREE flagship
    capital-ship types (build all three, hop between)" with dev's Enterprise / Defiant / Voyager
    setup. The 2026-09-18 leaning is instead ONE modular prototype hull that swaps modules for the
    three roles. Treat the modular-single-hull direction as the current lean and the three-separate-
    ships version as superseded-unless-the-user-revives-it. Confirm before building.

## System 3: ACTIVE MODE (unlocked with the flagship's first loop)
- A new spin on the **three base gameplay modes** (Tactical / Prospecting / Exploration), each as a
  themed **minigame** you actively play on the dreadnought. Passive mode is glorious (a better
  captain), but active mode is the point.

### Tactical -> turn-based RPG battle
- Tactician missions are replaced with a **turn-based RPG battle system**: choose targets, skills, etc.
- You choose a specialty mission and travel to it with a bit of fun flavor text, perhaps some
  **randomized story beats** along the way.
- The battle is **your ship + a few support vessels vs a small enemy fleet**, and it will NOT be a
  cakewalk. **Initially balanced for 3 ships**, with headroom to scale into missions / max difficulty
  levels for a **full fleet of 11 ships**.

### Prospecting -> asteroids minigame
- Only one ship, plus a system that lets you make this work more efficiently.
- The minigame is **asteroids with a twist**: you shoot ("pew pew") asteroids, they break up, and you
  **pull in the remains with a tractor beam**. Rewards land like any prospecting mission.
- **New rewards can appear in this mode.** (See the shared reward-layer rule below.)

### Exploration -> FrontierNav grid that PLAYS like a dungeon crawler (mostly withheld)
- The user will not reveal much yet "because exploration isn't in the game," and it makes fuller sense
  once 0.14.0 Exploration is known. But the shape is clarified (user, 2026-09-18):
- It **keeps the FrontierNav-style overhead-grid setup** (i.e. the SUGGESTIONS ~line 162 sketch stands,
  NOT replaced), and that setup **works SIMILARLY to a dungeon crawler**: as you move around the map,
  you get **flavor text about where you are standing and where you can go**.
- Explicit tone reference: an **homage to Wizardry**-style dungeon crawling, but deliberately **NOT
  stupidly hard like Wizardry 4**. Approachable, in keeping with the "build ease-of-use, sell peace"
  value.
- So "dungeon crawler" describes the FEEL of moving through the FrontierNav map (position-based flavor
  text + traversal), not a different structure. The two earlier framings are reconciled, not rival.

### Shared: two currencies (the global-contribution hook)
- Playing the minigames yields TWO currencies:
  1. A **minigame-improvement currency** for your abilities inside the minigames (user example: a
     drone swarm that actively picks up your rewards as you blow up asteroids).
  2. A **whole-game currency** that improves the entire game, so active play is not just for fun /
     more, it pays out globally. When the dreadnought is on passive mode, your (up to) 11 ships doing
     their various tasks do them **a bit better than before**: a full set of potent upgrades plus some
     **unique upgrades exclusive to this mode**.
- This directly satisfies the logged rule "a mode may have its own internal scaling, but it MUST also
  contribute to the global game" (SUGGESTIONS ~line 1819). Name the global contribution before
  building: here it is currency #2 feeding fleet-wide (passive) upgrades.
