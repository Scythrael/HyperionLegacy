## Root cause (at cffd5bf)

A weekend absence of 3d 1h:

1. On load, trusted time is capped at last verified + 2 days (trustedTime.ts, UNVERIFIED_ALLOWANCE_MS), so
   the reload paid exactly 2 days, a "~2d" popup appeared, and about 1d 1h was held.
2. When the server time check later succeeded, the poll owed about 1d 1h. `releaseHeld` cut it to the
   remaining budget (about 30 s), which is correct.
3. But `welcomeBack(beforeEconomy, plan.realElapsedSeconds)` opened a SECOND popup labeled "~1d 1h" while it
   paid about 30 s: the label used the uncut gap.

## What ships

1. **Offline cap 2 days to 4 days.** `OFFLINE_CAP_DAYS_BASE` moved to `src/lib/game/offlineCap.ts` (4),
   re-exported by tick.ts. Everything follows it: `offlineCapTicks` (still a function of state, the talent
   seam), the new `offlineCapSeconds(state)`, the trusted-time allowance (`UNVERIFIED_ALLOWANCE_MS =
   OFFLINE_CAP_SECONDS_BASE * 1000`, so a 3-day absence pays in full on load), the held-release budget, the
   recap's cap line and Help. Note: the trusted-time allowance is also the minigame's unverified clock
   allowance (it has no offline cap of its own), so it rises to 4 days with the main game's.
2. **Truthful recaps.** Every While You Were Away recap is labeled with the time actually PAID:
   `paidRealSeconds(plan, state)` (the cut plan, bounded by tick()'s cap) on the poll path, the polls'
   summed paid time on the hidden-tab path, and `min(offlineSeconds, cap)` on load. A release that paid
   less than the summary threshold raises nothing. `awayRecapSpan` extends an open recap by the live play
   before the new absence plus what that absence paid, never by the raw gap.
3. **Cap line.** One short line under the lead: "Offline progress pays up to 4d per absence." When the
   absence ran past the cap (more than a minute over), the lead reads "Your fleet ran for ~4d while you were
   away. Here is what it got done." and the line, in amber, reads "You were away ~5d. Offline progress pays
   up to 4d." The cap is read from `offlineCapSeconds(state)`, never hard-coded. Help gains an Offline
   Progress topic that states the cap from the constant.
4. APP_VERSION 0.13.11.3, a [PROPOSAL] patch note and `docs/patch-notes/discord-0.13.11.3.md`.

## Future work (not built)

- **Talent-upgradable offline cap.** The user wants the cap upgradable through talents at some point. The seam
  is `offlineCapTicks(state)` in tick.ts (`OFFLINE_CAP_DAYS_BASE + bonus days`); the recap and the release
  budget already read the per-state cap. The trusted-time allowance reads the BASE cap (trustedTime.ts has
  no state), so a raised cap would pay the bonus days on the next server confirmation rather than at load;
  when the talent is built, decide whether the allowance should follow the save's cap.
