THE e10,000,000 PRIZE · GRADIENT THEME OPTIONS

Four ways to read "panels and buttons get a diagonal gradient from the current color at the top left into purple at the right, translucent like today". Each is shown in the default cyan and in the green accent, to prove it follows whatever accent the player uses. The game is the same underneath; this is a cosmetic theme unlocked at e10,000,000 and picked in Settings > UI. You can also mix parts of different options.

A · Gradient wash

Every panel and button gets its own soft diagonal wash, accent to purple. The most literal reading. Calm and readable; everything matches.

B · Gradient edges

Panels and buttons keep their dark fill; only the borders, progress bars and primary buttons carry the accent-to-purple gradient. The most "premium" and the least busy.

C · One sweep across the screen

One continuous gradient runs across the whole screen, so panels on the left lean to your accent and panels on the right lean purple. It feels like one lit space rather than many pieces.

D · Wash plus shine

Option A plus gradient headings, gradient progress bars and a soft sheen on primary buttons. The flashiest: it clearly reads as a reward.
