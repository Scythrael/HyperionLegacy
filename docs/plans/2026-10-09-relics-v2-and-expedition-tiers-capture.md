radiant. However, (since they're mixed together by type. Perhaps a specific relic grouping. Groupings that might be

**My reading, to confirm at design time:**
- Finds are UNIDENTIFIED RELICS (a warehouse row), each with a rarity and an expedition TIER.
- A Research Lab tab IDENTIFIES them: each reveals a GENERAL relic from that tier's pool (about 4 per rarity band up
  to radiant, bucketed by dig-site groupings, weighted so some are far rarer), with a rare chance of an EXCEPTIONAL
  relic instead.
- Each TIER has its own unique exceptional SETS. Relic stats are significant and a full set is a massive boost
  (they do not stack with each other).
- T1 favors the weakest commons and has only 1 or 2 bonuses, unlocked very rarely; higher tiers roll better, up to
  the highest unlockable long-range expedition tier. Short missions drop relics too, less often.
- Collection is unbounded. A soft cap (example 1000) is where relic stats stop affecting the regular game; past it,
  collecting continues and a NEW line of endless-mode-only bonuses appears. Diminishing returns (D23) apply.
- A completed set earns a SYSTEM CHAT shoutout (chat is not built yet: log as a chat-event hook).

## 4. The 0.14.0 vision, re-stated by the user (verbatim, for scope reconciliation)

PART 1: Crew!
* Senior staff (basically, equip slots for crew members that also have an impact on the next bullet as department
  leads providing a bonus to those in their department). They provide unique buffs based on the crew members and
  their proficiencies.
* Crew compliment: the people running your ships! They all have very very tiny bonuses, but they add up. For example,
  one of the cruisers that will exist in the game (includes a small easter egg in the total) has a crew compliment of
  1,017. Split between departments, those 1,017 give a buff to the ship for past systems like combat and prospecting,
  but also for the other divisions like exploration, and other departments on the ship.
* Ground team: This is the team that goes down to a planet to perform specific planetary missions once long-range
  exploration is in the tasks phase. A team of security and science personnel. Security are there for ground combat.
  Science is there for the actual completion progression. Surveys, finding ancient ruins, guiding those explorations,
  scanning and categorizing flora and fauna, etc.
* 3 new ship equip slots!
  * Bridge: That's where your senior staff live. Provides bonuses based on the ship type you're flying, and then
    provides bonuses to your senior staff and their buffs to crew compliment.
  * Crew Quarters Module: Similar as the above. This focuses on buffs for the playstyle/ship type you're flying as
    well. More focused toward the specific crew compliment. Some might increase crew compliment, and others might
    impact the crew in other ways!
  * Sensor Suite: This is used throughout all exploration content. The shorter one-off missions might include
    scanning and cataloging something in nearby sectors. Or long-range expeditions will include a planetary system
    scanning phase where you identify every point of interest in an entire star system.

PART 2: Exploration!
* Exploration is sorta-kinda in 2 chunks. This is introducing a plan that I hadn't really started for Combat or
  Prospecting yet, so see this in the future. All prospecting and combat are currently in the same category as the
  next bullet point. Combat and Prospecting will both receive a completely new set of missions that are far closer to
  how expansive and different the second type of exploration content will work.
  * First type: Short-Range Missions (haven't locked this name down yet, so that's a WIP). These missions will be
    somewhat randomized compared to the set distance and so forth of other mission types, but very similar. You'll
    choose a mission like "bring refugees to a new camp on [insert planet here]. You'll have to be able to have enough
    room for that secondary personnel. So you might have a set of 100 refugees that you take to that planet. The
    distance itself will change based on the planetary distance, so transit time changes from run to run. Then you
    take time offloading them, then return to home. You'll see things like "pick up supplies/drop off supplies"
    missions with a similar setup. A small survey of some special phenomenon. A mission you go to repair a space
    telescope, or construct a comm signal booster. Things like that. It is semi random, and each time it runs, the
    constraints themselves will change. But each mission will require a specific level of various categories of stat,
    even if it's not using them. That way, if the mission randomizes, you can handle all random missions. So you might
    see missions requiring secondary personnel slots and required cargo bay slots. We're not storing refugees in the
    cargo bay. They have their own guest quarters aboard the ship. But if you're hauling supplies, you don't give them
    quarters.
  * The big one! (long-range expeditions; written separately earlier)
