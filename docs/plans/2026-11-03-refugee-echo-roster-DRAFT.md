# Refugee "franchise echo" roster: DRAFT for sign-off (2026-11-03)

Status: DRAFT. Every row is [PROPOSAL] until the user signs off. Nothing here is built. Slice: E5-6b (after E5-6),
per docs/plans/2026-11-02-refugee-posts-e5-design.md, section "USER (2026-11-03, verbatim): rare franchise nods
among refugees".

## Rules this draft follows

- Rarity: about 1 in 50 arrivals (about 2%), rolled on the arrival's seeded stream. The user's call.
- A "clear echo": an ORIGINAL name a fan recognizes at once, plus a wink in the one-line flavor. Never the character's
  exact name, never a trademarked term verbatim (no "Jedi", "Force", "Stargate", "Aes Sedai", "Saiyan", "Quirk",
  "Plus Ultra", "SOLDIER", "Wookiee" and so on). Echoes, puns, near-anagrams and descriptive nods only.
- No Star Trek ("there are enough nods to Star Trek that my other favorites deserve some love").
- They work like any refugee: one of the 8 backgrounds, the normal skill roll, no stronger buff. On the card: a small
  unique mark plus their own flavor line in place of the generic one.
- Background picked to fit the character (refiner, fuel handler, dispatcher, scholar, machinist, shipwright,
  salvager, stargazer).
- Tone: warm, peaceful, affectionate, never mocking. US English. No em dashes.
- The "Echoes" column is for the user's eyes only and is never shown in game.
- Refugees come from the player's homeworld, so non-human characters are echoed as people with that character's
  look, manner or habits.

Counts: Star Wars 8, Stargate 8, Babylon 5 8, Wheel of Time 7, Final Fantasy 7, Dragon Ball Z 8, My Hero Academia 8
(54 core). Optional "Etc." 5 (59 with all).
