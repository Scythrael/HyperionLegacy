My pick: the arrow. It buys the most room, and a left-pointing arrow at the top-left is a universally understood "back". The only cost is it drops the word "Facilities" — but the sub-tabs + panel already tell you where you are. If you'd rather keep a word, "Back" is the compromise (a bit wider, still far tighter than "← Facilities"). Tabs scroll on mobile only when a facility genuinely has too many to fit (Refinery's four); most fit outright (see Docks).
